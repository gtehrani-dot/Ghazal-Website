🚗 Self-drive Southwest loop

Overview
A four-day loop through some of the Southwest's biggest scenery — red rock, a canyon lake, and one of its most photogenic slot canyons.
Dates are anchored by the planning email itself, sent "Utah Itinerary :)" on Mon Aug 3, 2009, which lays the trip out from Wednesday through Saturday of that week — so Wed Aug 5 through Sat Aug 8, 2009.
DayFocus
