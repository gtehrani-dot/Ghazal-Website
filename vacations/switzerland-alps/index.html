Peaks & Excursions

Männlichen (2,343m)

Reached from Wengen via the Luftseilbahn Wengen-Männlichen cable car, or from Grindelwald via the Gondelbahn Grindelwald-Männlichen gondola — then a 15-minute walk to the summit. A popular viewpoint over the Lauterbrunnen valley and a starting point for hikers and skiers.

Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"

Departs from Interlaken, Grindelwald, or Wengen via cogwheel railway to Kleine Scheidegg (6,762 ft), then the Jungfrau railway to Eiger Glacier station, through the Eiger tunnel to the Jungfraujoch itself (over 11,000 ft — the highest railway station in Europe). Sightseeing includes the Great Aletsch Glacier (a 13.6-mile ice stream), the Ice Palace, and the Sphinx observation terrace. Booked via Rail Europe; exchange the voucher at the Interlaken Ost, Grindelwald, or Wengen station.

Schilthorn / Piz Gloria — Mürren

Round trip from Mürren by a four-segment cable car — the longest cable car system in the world. The revolving mountaintop restaurant at the top (site of the James Bond Bar, from filming "On Her Majesty's Secret Service") turns slowly over 200+ peaks; the "Martini, shaken not stirred" is the thing to order there.

Trümmelbach Falls — Lauterbrunnen

A mountain hollowed out by a series of waterfalls, accessible by elevator, then walkable back down. Buses run there, or it's a short hike from the Lauterbrunnen train station.
