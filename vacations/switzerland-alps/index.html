Overview
Two Swiss trips, remembered together as one: Lucerne, the Jungfrau region, Zermatt, and Lake Geneva.
This page combines what were actually two separate trips, at Ghazal's request, so every stop lives in one place: a Mürren/Bernese Oberland trip and a separate Zermatt/Matterhorn trip, both anchored by Lucerne and Montreux. Dates for the Mürren leg are estimated from photo timestamps (Nov 21–27, 2012); the surviving planning document is a set of mountain-excursion notes rather than a booked day-by-day itinerary. No dated record survives for the Zermatt leg, so it's included without specific calendar days. One detail we couldn't pin down: a train "to the glacier" with a stop connected to Lake Como, Italy — the real glacier train from Mürren (via Lauterbrunnen and Kleine Scheidegg to the Jungfraujoch) doesn't pass anywhere near Como, so that detail isn't reflected below. 🏔️ Mürren & the Bernese Oberland
Based in Wengen/Grindelwald/Mürren
Männlichen
  • Cable car from Wengen (Luftseilbahn Wengen-Männlichen) or gondola from Grindelwald (Gondelbahn Grindelwald-Männlichen), then a 15-minute walk to the 2,343m summit
  • Panoramic viewpoint over the Lauterbrunnen valley
Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"
  • The glacier train: departs Lauterbrunnen on the Wengernalp Railway to Kleine Scheidegg (2,061m), then the Jungfrau Railway through Eigergletscher (2,320m) and Eigerwand, stopping at Eismeer (3,158m — a window cut into the rock over the ice) before reaching the Jungfraujoch itself (3,454m/11,000+ ft) — the highest railway station in Europe
  • Views of the Great Aletsch Glacier (a 13.6-mile ice stream), the Ice Palace, and the Sphinx observation terrace
  • Voucher exchanged at Interlaken Ost, Grindelwald, or Wengen station
North Face Trail, Mürren
  • The North Face Trail (Nordwand Trail / Nordwandweg) — a cliff-edge terrace hike above the Lauterbrunnen valley, between Mürren and the hamlet of Gimmelwald
  • Uninterrupted views across the valley to the north faces of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau
Schilthorn / Piz Gloria — Mürren
  • Round trip from Mürren by a four-segment cable car — the longest cable car system in the world
  • The revolving mountaintop restaurant turns slowly over 200+ peaks; site of the James Bond Bar from "On Her Majesty's Secret Service" — the "Martini, shaken not stirred" is the thing to order
Trümmelbach Falls — Lauterbrunnen
  • A mountain hollowed out by a series of waterfalls, reached by elevator, then walkable back down
  • Buses run there, or it's a short hike from the Lauterbrunnen train station

Peaks & Excursions

Männlichen (2,343m)

Reached from Wengen via the Luftseilbahn Wengen-Männlichen cable car, or from Grindelwald via the Gondelbahn Grindelwald-Männlichen gondola — then a 15-minute walk to the summit. A popular viewpoint over the Lauterbrunnen valley and a starting point for hikers and skiers.

Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"

Departs from Interlaken, Grindelwald, or Wengen via cogwheel railway to Kleine Scheidegg (6,762 ft), then the Jungfrau railway to Eiger Glacier station, through the Eiger tunnel to the Jungfraujoch itself (over 11,000 ft — the highest railway station in Europe). Sightseeing includes the Great Aletsch Glacier (a 13.6-mile ice stream), the Ice Palace, and the Sphinx observation terrace. Booked via Rail Europe; exchange the voucher at the Interlaken Ost, Grindelwald, or Wengen station.

Schilthorn / Piz Gloria — Mürren

Round trip from Mürren by a four-segment cable car — the longest cable car system in the world. The revolving mountaintop restaurant at the top (site of the James Bond Bar, from filming "On Her Majesty's Secret Service") turns slowly over 200+ peaks; the "Martini, shaken not stirred" is the thing to order there.

Trümmelbach Falls — Lauterbrunnen

A mountain hollowed out by a series of waterfalls, accessible by elevator, then walkable back down. Buses run there, or it's a short hike from the Lauterbrunnen train station.

North Face Trail — Mürren

Cliff-edge terrace trail between Mürren and Gimmelwald, also called the Nordwand Trail or Nordwandweg. Continuous, unobstructed views of the north faces of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau across the valley.

Gornergrat — Zermatt

Cogwheel railway from Zermatt, one of the highest rack railways in Europe. Views of the Matterhorn and the surrounding glacier fields from the summit station.
