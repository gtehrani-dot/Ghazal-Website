Itinerary
Reconstructed from hotel and travel records, plus Ghazal's own account — not a confirmed day-by-day booking, but every stop is real.
Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"
  • The glacier train: departs Lauterbrunnen on the Wengernalp Railway to Kleine Scheidegg (2,061m), then the Jungfrau Railway through Eigergletscher (2,320m) and Eigerwand, stopping at Eismeer (3,158m — a window cut into the rock over the ice) before reaching the Jungfraujoch itself (3,454m/11,000+ ft) — the highest railway station in Europe
  • Views of the Great Aletsch Glacier (a 13.6-mile ice stream), the Ice Palace, and the Sphinx observation terrace
North Face Trail, Mürren
  • The North Face Trail (Nordwand Trail / Nordwandweg) — a cliff-edge terrace hike above the Lauterbrunnen valley, between Mürren and the hamlet of Gimmelwald
  • Uninterrupted views across the valley to the north faces of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau
Schilthorn / Piz Gloria — Mürren
  • Round trip from Mürren by a four-segment cable car — the longest cable car system in the world
  • The revolving mountaintop restaurant turns slowly over 200+ peaks; site of the James Bond Bar from "On Her Majesty's Secret Service" — the "Martini, shaken not stirred" is the thing to order
Zurich — Roche business
  • Ramada Zurich City, 2 nights, Tue Sep 25 – Thu Sep 27, 2012
  • Coordinated through Roche's Basel travel office (HRG Switzerland / Roche Hotel Center Basel)
Peaks & Excursions

Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"

Departs from Interlaken, Grindelwald, or Wengen via cogwheel railway to Kleine Scheidegg (6,762 ft), then the Jungfrau railway to Eiger Glacier station, through the Eiger tunnel to the Jungfraujoch itself (over 11,000 ft — the highest railway station in Europe). Sightseeing includes the Great Aletsch Glacier (a 13.6-mile ice stream), the Ice Palace, and the Sphinx observation terrace.

Schilthorn / Piz Gloria — Mürren

Round trip from Mürren by a four-segment cable car — the longest cable car system in the world. The revolving mountaintop restaurant at the top (site of the James Bond Bar, from filming "On Her Majesty's Secret Service") turns slowly over 200+ peaks; the "Martini, shaken not stirred" is the thing to order there.

North Face Trail — Mürren

Cliff-edge terrace trail between Mürren and Gimmelwald, also called the Nordwand Trail or Nordwandweg. Continuous, unobstructed views of the north faces of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau across the valley.
