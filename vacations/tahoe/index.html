Thu, Aug 6 · North Shore
Sand Harbor — your SUP day
🚗 10 min from Incline Village · leave by 7:45am 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
  • Arrive Sand Harbor by 8–8:30am — the lot fills and closes by mid-morning in August
  • SUP here — rent from Clearly Tahoe, the official on-site concessionaire inside the state park (right on the sand, no need to bring your own gear). 11' board runs $42/1hr up to $112 for a full day, 1-hour minimum. Sand Harbor itself doesn't allow outside delivery vendors since Clearly Tahoe holds the park concession
  • Picnic lunch on the beach, short walk out to the point for photos
  • 💡 Light tip: Sand Harbor is also one of the best sunset spots on the lake and needs no hike — an easy second visit tonight, just 10 minutes from your room
  • Optional: Thunderbird Lodge boat tour departing from Sand Harbor

Fri, Aug 7 · East Shore
Secret Cove, Zephyr Cove & Nevada Beach
🚗 20–35 min from Incline Village (Secret Cove 20, Zephyr Cove 30, Nevada Beach 35) · no fixed start, aim to leave by 1pm to land golden hour at the last stop 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
  • Pack lunch before you go — no food right at Secret Cove or Nevada Beach. Mountain High Sandwich Co in Incline Village is the move (gourmet deli, order ahead)
  • Secret Cove — stunning boulder cove; heads up, it's clothing-optional with a mixed crowd. Your call whether it's a swim stop or just a photo stop
  • Zephyr Cove Beach — wide sandy beach, restaurant/bar on site (can't-miss lunch or drinks right on the sand), watercraft rentals
  • Nevada Beach — quieter, walk-in, big mountain views
  • 💡 Light tip: the East Shore is at its best for golden-hour photos — the rock formations and boulders light up gold in the last hour before sunset. Worth timing your last stop for it, since you're driving back toward Incline anyway
  • 🎵 Music on the Beach note: Kings Beach's free concert is dark tonight — 2026 calendar skips Aug 7. Music night moves to Sunday (Day 5)

Peaceful day — Meeks Bay, then live music

Mon, Aug 10 · West Shore
Eagle Falls + Emerald Bay, then Bliss Beach
🚗 50–60 min from Incline Village to the trailhead — the longest drive of the trip · leave by 6–6:15am 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
  • 🍽 Grab coffee/pastries the night before or from Tahoe House Bakery in Tahoe City if it's open on your way through — Fire Sign Cafe (the local favorite) won't be open yet at this hour
  • Drive to the Eagle Falls / Emerald Bay trailhead — leave Incline by 6–6:15am to arrive by 7–7:30am, the small lot fills fast
  • 💡 Light tip: the early departure isn't just about parking — Emerald Bay at sunrise is the single most photographed view on the lake. Be at the overlook as the light comes up
  • Eagle Falls Trail — ~1.7 mi RT to the upper falls; extend to Eagle Lake (~4.4 mi RT) if the group's game
  • Emerald Bay / Vikingsholm Trail — ~1 mile down (real climb back up) to the historic mansion and beach
  • Cool off after at Bliss Beach (D.L. Bliss State Park) — some of the clearest water on the lake, a few minutes up the road, before the drive back to Incline
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner tonight: you've earned it — stop in Tahoe City on the way home for Wolfdale's (upscale Cal-Asian) or Sunnyside (lakefront deck) rather than driving all the way back first
