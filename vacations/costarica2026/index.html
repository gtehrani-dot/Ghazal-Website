The Springs Resort & Spa
Arenal · 3 Nights (Day 2 → Day 5)
~$996/night
📍 La Fortuna de San Carlos, Alajuela Province, Costa Rica 📞 +506 2401 3313 ✉️ reservations@thespringscostarica.com 🔖 Conf# not yet on file
165-acre five-star resort with spectacular Arenal Volcano views and 28 mineral-filled natural hot spring pools. CNN Travel named it one of the seven resorts with the most spectacular views in the world. Outstanding for families: waterslide, putt-putt course, kids' game room, and the on-site Club Rio Adventure Center with river tubing, kayaking, horseback riding, rock climbing, and an Animal Sanctuary. Five unique dining experiences including Las Ventanas, a dinner-only restaurant 1,000 feet above the ground with volcano views (reservations required, 5–10pm). Rated 9.8 for families. Breakfast at Tres Cascadas, served 6:30–10:30am — whether it's complimentary depends on the specific package booked, confirm at check-in.
🌋 Volcano Views · 28 Hot Springs · Family Paradise
Hotel Belmar
Monteverde · 2 Nights (Day 5 → Day 7)
~$620/night
📍 Calle Samudio, Barrio Copey, Monteverde, Puntarenas Province, Costa Rica 📞 +506 2645 5201 🔖 Conf# not yet on file
Mountain chalet-style ecolodge high in the cloud forest with a spring-fed pond, onsite microbrewery, and organic farm. The Peninsula Room has two queen beds and a private terrace with hammock — ideal for families. No pool, no A/C needed — Monteverde stays cool year-round and you're outside all day. Breakfast at Celajes Restaurant, served 6:30–10am — sources disagree on whether it's included in the rate or an à la carte add-on (~$16.50/person); confirm which applies to your booking.

Tulemar Resort
Manuel Antonio · 3 Nights (Day 7 → Day 10)
~$413/night
📍 Carretera a Manuel Antonio, Km 4, Quepos 60601, Puntarenas, Costa Rica 📞 +506 4001 5878 · 24hr front desk: 011-506-2777-9121 ✉️ Reservations@tulemar.com 🔖 Conf# not yet on file
A 33-acre gated jungle resort with private beach, multiple pools, and spectacular Manuel Antonio coastline views. Monkeys, birds, and sloths spotted from balconies daily. On-property sloth sanctuary. Beaches & Swimming — Manuel Antonio
Arenal and Monteverde are both inland, so all beach/swim options are here on the coast.

🏖️ Playa Biesanz

Calmest, most protected beach in the area. Tucked into a cove, almost no waves. Very safe for kids, excellent for snorkeling. Short hike keeps crowds low — local favorite.

🏖️ Playa Manuel Antonio

Inside the national park. Protected bay, gentle waves, beautiful and very safe swimming. You'll visit on your park day.

🏖️ Tulemar Private Beach

Calm, protected, exclusive to resort guests. The safest and most relaxed swimming option — right at the hotel with staff present.

🌊 Playa Espadilla

Just outside the park entrance — where surf lessons are held. More wave action, great for surfing. Swimmable but supervise your 10-year-old more carefully here.

ℹ️ Note: Arenal and Monteverde are both inland — no beach swimming in those regions. All water activities there are hot springs and river tubing.
