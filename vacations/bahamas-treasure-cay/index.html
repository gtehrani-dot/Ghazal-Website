Overview
A beach week on Treasure Cay's famous stretch of sand, traveling with the Was family, with plenty of boating out to nearby cays.
The surviving planning documents for this trip are a restaurant/boating reference sheet and an Abaco activities guide rather than a day-by-day itinerary, so this page captures the options and logistics rather than a fixed daily schedule. This was your third Bahamas trip — the first two went to Exuma (2010 and 2014, see their own pages), and this one shifted to Treasure Cay/Abaco.
