Why these dates

New moon is Mar 8, so all 10 nights have dark skies; March is also one of the most active aurora months (around the equinox).

Why three bases

Harads and Abisko are inland and drier, so clearer skies. Abisko sits in a rain shadow (the "blue hole"). The Norwegian coast is cloudier but the views are the most dramatic: two inland bases and one coastal base balance the odds.

Each afternoon

Check the yr.no cloud forecast and an aurora app (e.g. My Aurora Forecast). Clear sky matters more than the Kp number this far north.

Best hours

Darkness from ~7:30 PM; the strongest displays usually come 10 PM–1 AM.

If it's cloudy

Inland chases drive to clear sky: Lights over Lapland from Abisko, or the private car chase from Harstad ($553 for two).

Photos

A phone's night mode works; for a camera use a tripod, wide lens, ~3–8 sec at ISO 1600–3200. Keep spare batteries warm in an inside pocket.
