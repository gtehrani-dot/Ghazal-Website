Fri Mar 5 — Land in Luleå, drive to Arctic Bath

Arctic Bath · night 1 of 4 · Meals: SAS onboard / not planned — CPH/ARN airport / Arctic Bath Restaurant

  • Arrive
  • 10:35 AM — land Copenhagen, connect on SAS to Luleå (via Stockholm). Expect to land at Luleå Airport (LLA) mid-afternoon (exact time set at booking).
  • On landing — pick up the rental car at Hertz Luleå Airport & Self Service Kiosk . One-way to Kiruna Airport; desk open to midnight Fridays. Winter tyres standard.
  • ~1 hr 15 min drive north via Boden on road 97 to Arctic Bath . Check-in from 3 PM.
  • Evening
  • Spa ring: 2 saunas, steam, outdoor hot tubs and the open-air cold plunge in the frozen Lule River. Included.
  • Dinner — Arctic Bath floating restaurant unrated, $$$ . Prebooked with the room; tell them your landing time.
  • Aurora night 1 — 3 nights before new moon, dark skies. Step out from the cabin deck from ~7:30 PM.

Tue Mar 9 — Over the Arctic Circle to Abisko

  • 8:00 AM — breakfast, check out by 11 (leave by 8:45).
  • 8:45 AM — leave Arctic Bath. Road 97 → Jokkmokk → E45 Gällivare → E10 Kiruna, ~3 hr 40 min driving.
  • ~10:15 AM — Arctic Circle sign (Polcirkeln), road 97 , photo stop.
  • ~1:00 PM — lunch at Restaurang Ovttas (Nutti Sámi Siida) 4.8, $$ — Sámi-run, open fire, Arctic char and reindeer. 11 AM–3 PM.
  • ~2:00 PM — meet the reindeer at Nutti Sámi Siida – reindeer camp — Icehotel 37's ice art suites (day-visit tickets at the door).
  • Car off, transfer in
  • ~3:00 PM — drop the rental car at Kiruna Airport (KRN), rental car return (10 min away).
  • ~3:45 PM — Visit Abisko – Kiruna Airport → Abisko transfer (~105 min). Pick the first departure ≥30 min after your drop. The one daily train leaves Kiruna at 9:29 AM, too early.
  • ~5:30 PM — check in at Abisko Mountain Lodge , Superior Room. Breakfast and sauna included.
  • Dinner — Brasserie Fjällköket (Abisko Mountain Lodge) 4.7, $$$ at the lodge — 6-course local tasting.
  • Aurora night 5 — walk to the Lake Torneträsk shore (10 min). If it's cloudy, book the Lights over Lapland – Abisko Aurora Chase (7:45–11:45 PM) to drive to clear sky.

Sat Mar 13 — Drama day: whales and the Andøya Atlantic coast

WonderINN · night 3 of 4 · Meals: Cabin (early) / not planned — Andenes harbour / picnic / Cabin

  • North to Andenes
  • 7:00 AM — leave the cabin. ~3 hrs via Sortland to Andenes at the tip of Andøya.
  • ~10:30 AM — Whale Safari Andenes (RIB) – Whale2Sea — RIB out to the shelf edge where sperm whales feed close to shore. From NOK 1,690 pp (~$155). Winter departures are few: lock in the time when booking.
  • If no whale departure fits: Eagle safari Andenes – Whale2Sea (sea eagles, NOK 1,190 pp).
  • ~1:00 PM — Andenes lighthouse , lunch from the cabin or the harbour.
  • Scenic route home
  • ~2:00 PM — drive south on the Andøya Scenic Route (Bjørnskinn → Bleik → Andenes) beach walk, then Bukkekjerka rest area (Andøya Scenic Route) . Home ~6:30 PM.
  • Rocket launches from the Andøya spaceport can close the road for up to 8 hrs near Børvågen: check vegvesen.no the night before.
  • Dinner — cook at the cabin.
  • Aurora night 9.
