Overview
A ski week at the St. Regis Deer Valley with parents along, timed around Ghazal's birthday.
Dates here are our best reconstruction, not a firm record: the itinerary document was created and last edited Jan 18–24, 2015, a ski-rental confirmation was forwarded on Jan 17, 2015, and a "thank you for staying with us" survey arrived Jan 31, 2015 — consistent with a Sunday–Friday stay of Jan 25–30, 2015. (A separate St. Regis Deer Valley reservation exists on the calendar for Jan 29–Feb 4, 2020, but that same week is full of unrelated DC-area commitments — job interviews, jury duty, a comedy show, and a birthday dinner in Bethesda — so that 2020 booking doesn't look like it was actually used for this trip. This 2015 date is the best-supported match for the itinerary below.)
