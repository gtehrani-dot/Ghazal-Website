Reykjanes — the splurge

Private Retreat Lagoon, min. age 12, included with the room. Milky geothermal water set into a real lava field — the reference "splurge exception" from the taste profile. Lava View Junior Suite ~$2,400/night.

Design-forward, infinity-edge, ocean-view geothermal spa with the 7-step Skjól ritual — a deliberate contrast to the Blue Lagoon's mineral-mud vibe. Sér Pass (private locker room) from ~$135/person.

Honest odds

Northern Lights

October sits inside aurora season and benefits from an elevated solar cycle through 2026 — but it's also cloudier than September with shorter dark hours than deep winter. Roughly 60–65% odds of a sighting over a multi-night stay if you're actively checking clear skies. Treat it as a bonus, not the headline.

Reykjanes Peninsula, near the Blue Lagoon

Bridge Between Continents & Gunnuhver hot springs

A short walking bridge symbolically joining the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates, plus a steaming geothermal field nearby — both close to Keflavík. Real, well-known sights, but current review ratings weren't independently verified this pass — worth a quick check before adding.
