Itinerary
London → Istanbul
Fri Apr 30 – Sat May 1
Depart, overnight London, on to Istanbul
  • Overnight at the Hilton near Heathrow
  • Fly to Istanbul, check in at The W
Istanbul recommendations gathered ahead of the trip: Topkapı Palace Museum ("could have spent another day here"), the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern (underground cisterns), a Sufi/whirling dervish ceremony, the Grand Bazaar, and — a bit further out — the Hagia Sophia.
Turquoise Coast — self-drive
Tue May 4
Istanbul → Antalya → Çıralı
  • Fly Istanbul → Antalya (Atlasjet, flight KK14, 3:00–4:05)
  • Pick up rental car in Antalya, 4pm
  • Drive to Çıralı, near the town of Olympos
Wed May 5
Çıralı → Kaş → Kalkan
  • Stop in Kaş for shopping, lunch, and Kaputaş Beach (not staying overnight)
  • Continue to Kalkan for the night
Thu May 6
Saklıkent Gorge → Kabak / Butterfly Valley
  • Day trip to Saklıkent Gorge, lunch at Saklıkent Restaurant
  • Drive to Kabak, check out Butterfly Valley / Faralya, Kabak Bay (Kabak Köyü) beach
Fri May 7
Ölüdeniz
  • Ölüdeniz beach and lagoon
Sat May 8
Pamukkale (with Ephesus en route)
  • Stop at Ephesus (not staying overnight)
  • Pamukkale's travertine terraces
Sun May 9
Bodrum
  • Drop off rental car, Bodrum Downtown Office, 4pm
  • 🍽 Recommended locally: Sait and Deniz Ann restaurants
Greek islands
Mon May 10
Bodrum → Kos → Santorini (ferry)
  • Ferry Bodrum → Kos, then Kos → Santorini
  • Check in at Iliovasilema Suites, Imerovigli
