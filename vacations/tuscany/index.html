🍷 Autumn Trip · Oct 31 – Nov 7, 2015

TuscanyWine country by day, a villa and the St. Regis Florence by night

🗓 7 nights · Oct 31 – Nov 7
📍 Sinalunga → Montepulciano/Montalcino → Siena → Florence → Parma
🏡 Locanda dell'Amorosa then St. Regis Florence
✈️ Home via Milan
📷 The rolling hills near Montalcino
Overview
A week driving through wine country — Montepulciano and Montalcino — before ending in Florence and a scenic route home through Parma.

Route

Self-drive road trip through wine country, ending with a fly-out from Milan.
Itinerary
Day 1 · Sat, Oct 31 · Arrival
Locanda dell'Amorosa
  • Check in at Locanda dell'Amorosa, Sinalunga — 11am arrival
  • Chill day inside — sleep, read, settle in
Day 2 · Sun, Nov 1
Montepulciano · Val d'Orcia · Pienza
  • Wine tasting and lunch at Salcheto (Via di Villa Bianca 15, Montepulciano, hours 10–6)
  • Explore castle-hotels and the town of Montepulciano
Day 3 · Mon, Nov 2
Montalcino wine country
  • Wine tasting at Poggio Antico, 10:30am (Montalcino, Siena · +39 0577-848044)
  • Drive-through of Biondi Santi, castle hotels
  • Other wineries researched for this day: Sesti Castello di Argiano, Uccelliera, Casa Raia — and the Terme di Saturnia hot springs was on the list to consider
Day 4 · Tue, Nov 3
Siena → Chianti → Cortona → Florence
  • Check out of Locanda dell'Amorosa
  • Siena Cathedral, Piazza del Campo — aperitif on the piazza
  • San Gimignano 1300, sunset at Panzano (hilltop Chianti village), Cortona spa
  • Chianti wineries considered: Antinori Chianti Classico, Castello Ama
  • Early evening: check in to St. Regis Florence
Day 5 · Wed, Nov 4
Florence — cooking & food tour
  • Florence for Foodies walking tour, 9:30am–1:30pm — starts in the San Lorenzo neighborhood and the central market (info@florenceforfoodies.com)
  • 🍽 Evening cooking class, 5:30pm, at Villa Pandolfini (Via Livornese 334, Firenze)
Day 6 · Thu, Nov 5
Florence — museums & galleries
  • Uffizi Gallery, Piazzale Michelangelo, Piazza della Signoria, Basilica San Miniato al Monte, Accademia Gallery
  • Considered: an evening screening on the Medici at San Giovannino dei Padri Scolopi Library (Piazza di San Lorenzo 6)
Day 7 · Fri, Nov 6
Lucca → Parma
  • Check out of St. Regis Florence
  • Le mura di Lucca — the city walls walk (weather permitting)
  • Drive to Parma; evening check-in north of Parma at Polesine Parmense
Day 8 · Sat, Nov 7 · Departure
Fly home from Milan
  • Breakfast, check out
  • Fly home via Milan (Malpensa/Linate)
