Stops & Excursions

Gornergrat — Zermatt

Cogwheel railway from Zermatt, one of the highest rack railways in Europe. Views of the Matterhorn and the surrounding glacier fields from the summit station.

Chapel Bridge — Lucerne

Kapellbrücke, a covered wooden footbridge over the Reuss river in Lucerne's old town — one of Europe's oldest surviving truss bridges.

CERN — Meyrin, Geneva

European Organization for Nuclear Research, home of the Large Hadron Collider. Visitor exhibits on particle physics, including the Globe of Science and Innovation.

Lake Geneva promenade — Montreux

Lakefront walk with views back toward the mountains.
