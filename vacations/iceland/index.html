Trip Overview
Every day at a glance — the full detail lives in the Itinerary tab.
Why this route: A counter-clockwise loop out of Keflavík so the crowded Golden Circle and South Coast come first while jet lag keeps mornings early, glacier country and the East Fjords fill the quiet middle days, and the trip closes with Snæfellsnes and one splurge night at the Blue Lagoon on the way back to the airport — no backtracking anywhere on the route.

Flag for you: September puts you in shoulder season — mild temps, lower prices than summer peak, and a real (not guaranteed) shot at the northern lights once it's dark enough after ~9:30pm. It's also active weather season on the Ring Road; the East Fjords and Mývatn legs (Days 5–7) are the ones most likely to need a plan-B if wind or rain rolls in.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Counter-clockwise loop from Keflavík — South Coast first while jet lag keeps mornings early, glacier country and the quiet East Fjords in the middle, Snæfellsnes and the Blue Lagoon on the way back to the airport.

Golden Circle
  • Þingvellir National Park — walk the rift valley between the tectonic plates; easy, flat trails good for both kids.

  • 🍽 Lunch: Friðheimar 4.6, $$ — dine inside a working tomato greenhouse, unlimited tomato soup and fresh-baked bread, a genuine kid favorite.
On to Vík
  • ~2.5 hr drive south to Vík, check in to Hotel Katla.
  • 🍽 Dinner: Suður-Vík 4.4, $$ — ocean-view restaurant in a historic house above the village.

Day 3 · Mon, Sep 7
South Coast waterfalls & the black sand beach
🚗 ~1.5 hrs driving total · leave by 9:00am
🌤️ Weather · Temp: 45–52°F · Precip: 40% chance showers · Wind: 20–30 mph (coastal) Historical
🍳 Breakfast — hotel 🥪 Lunch — Skógakaffi (Skógar) 🍽 Dinner — Black Beach Restaurant
  • Seljalandsfoss — the waterfall you can walk behind; bring a rain jacket for the kids, they'll want to do the loop twice.
  • Skógafoss — massive, straight-on falls with a steep staircase to a viewing platform at top, real hike energy for the 13-year-old.
  • 🍽 Lunch: Skógakaffi 4.3, $ — casual café right at the Skógafoss parking area.
  • Reynisfjara black sand beach — basalt columns and sea stacks; stay well back from the water, sneaker waves here are genuinely dangerous.
  • Dyrhólaey — the arch and puffin colony viewpoint (puffins gone by September, but the cliffs and views hold up).
  • 🍽 Dinner: Black Beach Restaurant 4.2, $$$ — at Reynisfjara, known for its lobster soup; splurge pick for the day.

Day 4 · Tue, Sep 8
Glacier hike at Skaftafell, then Jökulsárlón & Diamond Beach
🚗 ~2.5 hrs driving total · leave by 9:00am
🌤️ Weather · Temp: 43–50°F · Precip: light · Wind: 20–25 mph, colder near the glacier Historical
🍳 Breakfast — hotel 🥪 Lunch — Systrakaffi (Kirkjubæjarklaustur) 🍽 Dinner — Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon restaurant
  • 🍽 Lunch: Systrakaffi 4.3, $ — small-town café roughly halfway, good rest stop before the park.
  • Skaftafell — Vatnajökull National Park — book a family glacier walk (crampons + guide provided, ~3 hrs, suitable for both kids' ages) or self-guide the Svartifoss trail if you'd rather skip the guided tour.
  • Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon — icebergs calving into the lagoon; a Zodiac boat tour among the ice is worth booking ahead in September.
  • Diamond Beach — ice chunks washed up on black sand directly across the road from the lagoon.
  • 🍽 Dinner: hotel restaurant at Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon 4.1, $$ — this stretch is remote, dinner options are limited to the hotel.
  • Alternate if the glacier walk is sold out: swap in the Svartifoss basalt-column waterfall hike instead, similar difficulty and time.

Day 6 · Thu, Sep 10
Dettifoss, Krafla, and the new Earth Lagoon Mývatn
🚗 ~3.5 hrs driving total · leave by 9:00am
🌤️ Weather · Temp: 41–50°F · Precip: light · Wind: 15–20 mph, drier interior climate Historical
🍳 Breakfast — hotel 🥪 Lunch — Vogafjós Cowshed Café 🍽 Dinner — Vogafjós Cowshed Café
  • Dettifoss — Europe's most powerful waterfall by volume, a short but rocky/uneven walk from the car park.
  • 🍽 Lunch & dinner: Vogafjós Cowshed Café 4.5, $$ — eat literally next to the barn's dairy cows, a genuine novelty for kids and consistently good local food; worth returning to for both meals today.
  • Krafla & Víti crater — volcanic caldera with a turquoise crater lake, easy short walk.
  • Earth Lagoon Mývatn (formerly Myvatn Nature Baths, reopened Jul 2026 after a full rebuild) — geothermal soak with volcanic-crater views; Earth Signature tier (~$80/adult) includes a towel + drink. Hours can still be settling post-reopening — confirm before you go.

Day 9 · Sun, Sep 13
Snæfellsnes Peninsula loop
🚗 ~2 hrs driving total · leave by 9:30am
🌤️ Weather · Temp: 44–52°F · Precip: showers possible · Wind: 20–25 mph Historical
🍳 Breakfast — hotel 🥪 Lunch — Bjargarsteinn Mathús (Grundarfjörður) 🍽 Dinner — Fjöruhúsið
  • Kirkjufell — Iceland's most photographed mountain, with Kirkjufellsfoss waterfall right at its base; short easy walk.
  • 🍽 Lunch: Bjargarsteinn Mathús 4.5, $$ — seafood with a Kirkjufell view from the dining room, kid-friendly menu.

  • Snæfellsjökull National Park — the glacier-capped volcano that inspired Jules Verne's "Journey to the Center of the Earth"; viewpoints only today, no summit attempt.
  • 🍽 Dinner: Fjöruhúsið 4.6, $$ — tiny fish shack in Hellissandur, famous for its fish soup; walk-in only, no booking needed.

Honest odds

Northern Lights

Early September sits right at the start of aurora season — dark enough after ~9:30pm, but not guaranteed. Best shot is a clear night at Mývatn or Snæfellsnes, both dark-sky locations away from Reykjavík's light pollution. Check the Icelandic Met Office aurora forecast each evening once you're on the ground — no way to book this in advance.
