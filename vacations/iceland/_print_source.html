Day-by-Day

Day 1 · Sat, Sep 5 — Land at Keflavík, easy first day in Reykjavík

  • Land KEF ~7:00am, pick up rental car, drive to Reykjavík (~45 min)No timed activities before hotel check-in
  • Lunch: Bæjarins Beztu Pylsur, harbor hot dog cart
  • Walk: Hallgrímskirkja church tower, Sun Voyager sculpture
  • Dinner: Messinn, downtown — reservation recommended

Day 2 · Sun, Sep 6 — Golden Circle, then Vík

  • Leave Reykjavík by 8:30amÞingvellir → Geysir → Gullfoss, ~2.5 hrs driving between stops
  • Lunch: Friðheimar tomato farm, Reykholt
  • Drive to Vík (~2.5 hrs), check in to Hotel Katla
  • Dinner: Suður-Vík

Day 3 · Mon, Sep 7 — South Coast waterfalls & Reynisfjara

  • Leave Vík by 9:00amSeljalandsfoss → Skógafoss → Reynisfjara → Dyrhólaey
  • Lunch: Skógakaffi, Skógar
  • Caution: stay well back from the water at Reynisfjara — sneaker waves
  • Dinner: Black Beach Restaurant, Reynisfjara

Day 4 · Tue, Sep 8 — Skaftafell glacier hike, Jökulsárlón, Diamond Beach

  • Leave Vík by 9:00amLunch en route: Systrakaffi, Kirkjubæjarklaustur
  • Skaftafell family glacier walk (crampons + guide, ~3 hrs) — book ahead
  • Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon — optional Zodiac boat tour among the icebergs
  • Diamond Beach, across the road from the lagoon
  • Dinner: Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon restaurant (limited options, remote area)

Day 5 · Wed, Sep 9 — Höfn lunch, East Fjords to Egilsstaðir

  • Leave by 9:00amLower-key travel day between two adventure days
  • Lunch: Pakkhús Restaurant, Höfn — langoustine, walk-in only
  • Scenic East Fjords drive, detour to Seyðisfjörður (~30 min extra)
  • Dinner: Kaffi Hérað, Egilsstaðir

Day 6 · Thu, Sep 10 — Dettifoss, Krafla, Earth Lagoon Mývatn

  • Leave by 9:00amDettifoss (uneven walk from car park) → Krafla/Víti crater
  • Lunch & dinner: Vogafjós Cowshed Café, Mývatn
  • Earth Lagoon Mývatn — reopened Jul 2026, confirm hours before visiting

Day 7 · Fri, Sep 11 — Húsavík whale watching & GeoSea, base Akureyri

  • Leave by 9:00amDrive to Húsavík (~1 hr)
  • Whale watching — North Sailing or Gentle Giants, ~3 hrs, book ahead
  • Lunch: Salka Restaurant, Húsavík harbor
  • GeoSea Geothermal Baths, Húsavík
  • Drive to Akureyri (~50 min), dinner: Rub23

Day 8 · Sat, Sep 12 — Long transfer to Snæfellsnes

  • Leave by 9:30amLongest single drive of the trip, ~4.5 hrs via Route 1 / Route 54
  • Lunch: Settlement Center Café, Borgarnes
  • Dinner: Hotel Búðir restaurant — reserve ahead

Day 9 · Sun, Sep 13 — Snæfellsnes Peninsula loop

  • Leave by 9:30amKirkjufell → Djúpalónssandur → Arnarstapi → Snæfellsjökull viewpoints
  • Lunch: Bjargarsteinn Mathús, Grundarfjörður
  • Dinner: Fjöruhúsið, Hellissandur — walk-in only, no reservation needed

Day 10 · Mon, Sep 14 — Drive back to Reykjavík, free afternoon

  • Leave by 10:00amLunch: Settlement Center Café, Borgarnes (return stop)
  • Free afternoon: Laugavegur, harbor area, or a city geothermal pool
  • Dinner: Sjávargrillið, downtown Reykjavík

Day 11 · Tue, Sep 15 — Blue Lagoon splurge night

  • Leave Reykjavík by 1:00pmRetreat check-in from 3:00pm
  • Retreat Spa treatment (optional, min age 12) or public Blue Lagoon (min age 2, supervised)
  • Dinner: Lava Restaurant — reserve alongside the Retreat stay

Day 12 · Wed, Sep 16 — Morning at the lagoon, fly home

  • Checkout 11:00am20 min drive to KEF, return rental car
  • Depart KEF midday, direct to IAD
