Overview
The first of two Exuma trips — a villa week built around boat days to nearby cays and beaches.
This was the earliest of your three Bahamas trips, five years before Treasure Cay. The planning spreadsheet compared villas around Georgetown and Little Exuma, plus a shortlist of beaches and cays to reach by boat or car — it doesn't lay out a day-by-day schedule, so this page captures the research and the villa you settled on rather than a fixed itinerary.

Villa & Boats
Lodging

Done Reach Villa — Mariah Harbour, Little Exuma

Hot tub, near the dock, kayaks and an 11-ft Boston Whaler included for Mariah Harbour and nearby beaches, a hammock near the water, 2 outdoor showers. Listed around $3,000/week, negotiated down and accepted at $2,000.
Other villas considered

Near Georgetown, dock on-site, good reviews — $2,200+, offered down to $1,975
