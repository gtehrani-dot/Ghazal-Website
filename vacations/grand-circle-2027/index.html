This trip needs up to 15 months of advance planning

Several can’t-miss pieces are permit- or lottery-only and open long before the trip. Miss the window and they’re gone.

  • Grand Canyon mule ride — 15 months — Opens 15 months ahead and sells out. June 2027 is already bookable (Xanterra, 888-297-2757).
  • The Wave — 4 months (lottery) — Apply Feb 1–28, 2027 for June; draw Mar 1. Backup: the daily lottery from Page, 2 days ahead (16 people/day).
  • Havasupai Falls — book early in the year of the trip — Tribal permits only, 3 nights minimum. Season permits have gone on sale early in the year and sell out fast; confirm the 2027/2028 release on havasupaireservations.com. Not on this itinerary.
  • Hell’s Backbone Grill — ~3 months — Reserve the day its 2027 season calendar opens (2026 opened Mar 14).
  • Angels Landing — ~2½ months (lottery, optional) — Seasonal lottery about Apr 1–20, 2027. Scout Lookout needs no permit.

Grand Canyon · Havasupai Reservation · separate add-on

Havasupai Falls (Havasu Canyon)

Camp below the blue-green Havasu, Mooney and Beaver falls on Havasupai land in a side canyon of the Grand Canyon. Permits: every stay is 4 days / 3 nights, booked only through the tribe (2026: campground $455/person; lodge $2,277/room for up to 4; kids 6 and under free, 7+ need a permit). Getting there: check in at Grand Canyon Caverns & Inn (Havasupai check-in) the day before, then hike ~8 mi and ~2,000 ft down from Hualapai Hilltop Trailhead to Supai Village and ~2 more to the campground. Pack mules $400 round trip (book 72 hrs ahead); the helicopter ($300/person one way) is first-come and not reliable. Timing: peak season is May–Sep and monsoon is Jul–Sep (flash floods), so April–May or late September–October is the sweet spot; late June means a pre-dawn hike out in 100°F heat. Cancellations get 50% back only 90+ days out. Adds 4–5 days, so it’s a trip of its own rather than a Grand Circle stop.
