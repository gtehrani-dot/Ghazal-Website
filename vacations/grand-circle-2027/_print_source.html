Day-by-Day

Day 1 · Fri, Jun 18 · Travel day — Fly into Las Vegas, Valley of Fire, east to Zion

~4h of driving: LAS → Valley of Fire 1h · → Hurricane 2h · → Glendale via Zion 1h15 · Sleep: A-frame near Zion & Bryce · Hot Tub & Cold Plunge
  • Fly DC → Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) (nonstops exist from IAD/DCA); aim to land by ~10:30 AM. Pick up the one-way SUV.
  • 11:30 AM — Lunch: Esther's Kitchen — seasonal pasta and sourdough, 15 min from the airport and next to the I-15 on-ramp.
  • 1:30 PM — Valley of Fire State Park — enter at the west gate with cash for the $15 fee; air-conditioned visitor center 9–4.
  • Short stops: Atlatl Rock (stairs to petroglyphs), Mouse's Tank (Petroglyph Canyon) (0.75 mi flat), Rainbow Vista and Elephant Rock on the way out.
  • Alt: Ice cream after: The Inside Scoop in Overton, 15 min from the east entrance.
  • 6:15 PM MT — Dinner: Pig's Ear American Bistro in Hurricane — burgers, seafood, pasta; the best-rated table near Zion.
  • Alt: Casual swap: River Rock Roasting Co. — pizza on a deck over the Virgin River gorge.
  • 7:30 PM — Pick up Narrows gear at Zion Outfitter (Narrows gear) (open till 8; evening pickup from 4 PM saves the morning).
  • 8:15 PM — Drive the Zion–Mt Carmel Highway through the park and the tunnel at dusk to A-frame near Zion & Bryce · Hot Tub & Cold Plunge in Glendale. Hot tub.

Day 2 · Sat, Jun 19 · Zion — Canyon Overlook at sunrise, then wade the Narrows

~45 min Glendale → Zion Canyon Visitor Center (via the tunnel) · Leave by 5:45 AM · Sleep: A-frame near Zion & Bryce · Hot Tub & Cold Plunge
  • 6:05 AM — Canyon Overlook Trail — 1 mi round trip on the east side of the tunnel; best at sunrise, tiny lot.
  • 7:00 AM — Park at the visitor center (fills by 9) and ride the shuttle to the last stop.
  • 7:45 AM — The Narrows (bottom-up) · Temple of Sinawava — the 1-mi paved Riverside Walk, then wade upstream as far as the kids like; turn around at 2–3 hrs. Wall Street is ~2 mi up.
  • If the Narrows is closed: Kanarra Falls (ticket required, 50 min away) or Emerald Pools Trail (Zion Lodge) plus Scout Lookout (Angels Landing trail) instead.
  • 12:30 PM — Lunch: The Hangry Goat — BBQ brisket bowls in Springdale.
  • Alt: Swap: Oscar's Cafe — big Tex-Mex plates on a patio.
  • 2:00 PM — Emerald Pools Trail (Zion Lodge) — Lower to Middle pools (2.2 mi), walk behind waterfall mist. Or wade at the Pa'rus Trail by Canyon Junction.
  • Alt: Bigger hike: Scout Lookout (Angels Landing trail) — Walter’s Wiggles to the Angels Landing viewpoint, no chains, no permit (~3 hr). Angels Landing itself needs a lottery permit and has serious exposure.
  • Alt: Warm-water swim instead: Sand Hollow State Park — red-sand beach, 40 min west.
  • 5:30 PM — Dinner: King's Landing Bistro — king salmon, duck; Springdale’s best (Tue–Sat).
  • Alt: East-side dinner: Sego in Kanab, 30 min from Glendale.
  • 7:30 PM — Alt: Watchman Trail at golden hour, or just the Canyon Junction bridge view of the Watchman peak.

Day 3 · Sun, Jun 20 · Bryce Canyon — Down among the hoodoos, Mossy Cave waterfall, sunset at Bryce Point

~1h Glendale → Bryce Canyon · Leave by 7:30 AM · Sleep: Stone Canyon Inn · 2BR Cabin
  • Alt: Early-bird option: sandboard at Coral Pink Sand Dunes State Park 7–9 AM before the sand heats up (adds ~1 hr of driving).
  • 8:45 AM — Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop (Sunrise Point) — down Queen’s Garden, up Navajo Loop through Wall Street, out at Sunset Point (~2.5 hr). The classic family loop.
  • Alt: On horseback instead: Canyon Trail Rides (Bryce horseback) — Queen’s Garden ride at 9 AM or the 3-hr Peek-a-boo ride (both kids qualify at 10+).
  • Alt: Quieter trail: Fairyland Point out-and-back, or Rainbow Point · Bristlecone Loop at the end of the scenic drive.
  • 12:00 PM — Picnic lunch on the rim (Bryce food options are thin and under the bar).
  • 2:30 PM — Mossy Cave Trail — 0.8 mi to a small waterfall and a creek to wade. No park fee here.
  • 3:30 PM — Check in at Stone Canyon Inn · 2BR Cabin: cabin with its own deck hot tub.
  • 6:00 PM — Dinner on site: Stone Hearth Grille — art-filled room with Bryce views. Reserve.
  • 8:15 PM — Sunset at Bryce Point (or Inspiration Point). Bryce has some of the darkest skies in the country — step outside after 10.

Day 4 · Mon, Jun 21 · Scenic Byway 12 — Swim under Lower Calf Creek Falls, ride the Hogback, dinner at Hell’s Backbone

~3h of driving: Tropic → Calf Creek 1h15 · → Boulder 20 min · → Torrey 1h10 · Leave by 6:15 AM · Sleep: Skyview Hotel Torrey · Premium Hot Tub Room
  • 6:15 AM — Coffee and pastries to go at Bryce Canyon Coffee Co. (opens 7; or pack breakfast).
  • Alt: Pick up pizza and sandwiches for a picnic at Escalante Outfitters in Escalante (opens 11 — only works if you hike second).
  • 7:30 AM — Lower Calf Creek Falls — ~6 mi round trip on sand (2.5–3 hr) to a 126-ft waterfall and a cold, swimmable pool with a shady beach. Lot fills by 9–10.
  • 11:45 AM — Drive The Hogback (Scenic Byway 12) — a knife-edge ridge road with drops on both sides.
  • 12:15 PM — Anasazi State Park Museum in Boulder — reconstructed pueblo and a cool museum during the hottest hours.
  • 4:00 PM — Early dinner: Hell's Backbone Grill & Farm — farm-to-table from its own farm. Reserve the 4 PM seating.
  • 5:30 PM — Drive over Boulder Mountain (UT-12, 9,600 ft) to Torrey, 1h10.
  • 6:45 PM — Check in at Skyview Hotel Torrey · Premium Hot Tub Room (front desk 3–10 PM): room with a private patio hot tub and a rooftop for stars.
  • 8:30 PM — Sunset at Sunset Point & Goosenecks Overlook (Capitol Reef), 10 min away.
  • If HBG is full: Chak Balam in Torrey, open Mondays.

Day 5 · Tue, Jun 22 · Drive to Monument Valley — Capitol Reef at sunrise, the Moki Dugway, sunset in Monument Valley

~4h30 of driving: Torrey → Hanksville 1h · → Moki Dugway 2h30 · → Monument Valley 1h · Leave by 6:00 AM · Sleep: The View Hotel · Premium Cabin
  • 6:15 AM — Hickman Bridge Trail — 1.8 mi to a 133-ft natural bridge you walk beneath; empty at sunrise.
  • Alt: Also great: Grand Wash (shady dry narrows), Capitol Gorge & The Tanks (pioneer signatures) or Cassidy Arch (stand on the arch).
  • 9:00 AM — Gifford Homestead (Capitol Reef) — mini fruit pies and cinnamon rolls the minute it opens; orchard cherries/apricots may be in season.
  • Alt: Sit-down breakfast in Torrey instead: The Wild Rabbit Cafe or Shooke Coffee Roasters (from 7:30).
  • 10:45 AM — Early lunch and fuel: Duke's Slickrock Grill in Hanksville.
  • 12:30 PM — Hite Overlook (Lake Powell) over the top of Lake Powell on UT-95.
  • Alt: Add Natural Bridges National Monument (9-mi loop) if everyone’s fresh.
  • 2:15 PM — Muley Point — hidden-gem rim view of the San Juan goosenecks with Monument Valley on the horizon.
  • 2:45 PM — Down the Moki Dugway switchbacks, then Valley of the Gods loop (or part of it).
  • 3:45 PM — Goosenecks State Park, then Forrest Gump Point for the classic road photo.
  • 4:45 PM — Check in at The View Hotel · Premium Cabin — porch facing the Mittens. Pay the tribal park fee ($10/person + $15/vehicle; passes not accepted) at Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park.
  • 5:30 PM — Navajo-guided sunset tour with Navajo Spirit Tours (Monument Valley) — reaches arches and petroglyphs closed to private cars.
  • 8:15 PM — Dinner: Goulding's Stagecoach Dining Room — Navajo tacos and frybread. Nothing nearby clears 4.5; this is the best of what exists.

Day 6 · Wed, Jun 23 · Monument Valley → Page — Mittens at sunrise, into Lower Antelope Canyon, Lake Powell beach

~2h Monument Valley → Page (gain an hour: Arizona time) · Leave by 7:30 AM · Sleep: “Balcony House” guest suite with private hot tub
  • 5:30 AM — Sunrise from the cabin porch, then Wildcat Trail (Mittens loop) around West Mitten (sign in at the visitor center; sunrise only — it’s fully exposed).
  • 8:30 AM MT — Drive to Page (~2 hr; clocks go back an hour).
  • 10:00 AM AZ — Lower Antelope Canyon with Ken's Tours · Lower Antelope Canyon — ladders and narrow passages; the most fun slot for kids.
  • Alt: Same canyon, other operator: Dixie's Lower Antelope Canyon Tours. Iconic light beams: Antelope Canyon Navajo Tours · Upper Antelope at 11:40. Least crowded: Taadidiin Tours · Antelope Canyon X.
  • 12:00 PM — Lunch: BirdHouse — fried chicken famous across Arizona.
  • 1:00 PM — Check in at “Balcony House” guest suite with private hot tub — private hot tub on the balcony. Siesta through the heat.
  • 5:30 PM — Swim at Lone Rock Beach (Lake Powell) — warm lake water on a drive-on beach (deep sand; park on firm ground and walk). Bring shade.

Day 7 · Thu, Jun 24 · Lake Powell — Horseshoe Bend at sunrise, kayak into Antelope Canyon, Navajo dinner show

Short hops around Page · Leave by 4:50 AM · Sleep: “Balcony House” guest suite with private hot tub
  • 5:10 AM — Horseshoe Bend at sunrise — 1.5 mi round trip, $10 parking; no shade or water later.
  • 6:15 AM — Donuts and coffee at Hot n Sweet Coffee & Donut.
  • 7:00 AM — Antelope Canyon by kayak with Lake Powell Paddleboards & Kayaks — paddle up the flooded canyon from Antelope Point Public Launch Ramp, then hike into the dry slot (~4 hr). Early slot beats the afternoon wind.
  • 12:30 PM — Pool/hot tub and rest through the heat.
  • 4:30 PM — Glen Canyon Dam Overlook — free, 5-minute scramble to the canyon rim.
  • Alt: Sunset hike instead: Toadstool Hoodoos — balanced-rock hoodoos 45 min west (1.8 mi, easy).
  • 6:00 PM — Dinner and show: Red Heritage (Navajo dinner & show) — frybread tacos, then Native dance at 7. Book ahead.
  • Alt: One-night splurge: move tonight to Amangiri (~25 min away; from ~$4,200).

Day 8 · Fri, Jun 25 · To the Grand Canyon — In the East Entrance: Desert View, Shoshone Point, sunset at Hopi Point

~2h15 Page → Desert View · ~45 min rim → Williams A-frame · Leave by 8:00 AM · Sleep: A-Frame: Milky Way + Desert Views
  • If you won The Wave lottery: dawn hike at The Wave (Coyote Buttes North) (6.4 mi, no shade, 4+ liters each), then drive 3.5 hr to the rim. No permit? Wire Pass → Buckskin Gulch is the slot-canyon backup.
  • 7:15 AM — Breakfast at Hot n Sweet Coffee & Donut, then south on US-89.
  • 10:15 AM — Desert View Watchtower — climb the 1932 tower at the East Entrance.
  • 12:00 PM — Lunch: Canyon Adventures Café (Visitor Center plaza) at the visitor center plaza (grab-and-go; under the bar).
  • 1:00 PM — Mather Point — the iconic first view.
  • 2:00 PM — Shoshone Point — hidden gem: an easy 2-mi forest walk to a point with no crowds.
  • 4:00 PM — Mule-ride check-in/weigh-in at Canyon Vistas Mule Ride (Xanterra) (Bright Angel Lodge) for tomorrow.
  • 6:15 PM — Dinner: El Tovar Dining Room — 1905 lodge on the rim; book at the 60-day mark (under the bar; here for the room).
  • 7:30 PM — Red shuttle to Hopi Point for sunset (~7:45 PM). Then 45 min to the A-Frame: Milky Way + Desert Views — private sauna.

Day 9 · Sat, Jun 26 · Grand Canyon — Hike below the rim at dawn, ride the rim on a mule

~45 min each way A-frame ↔ South Rim · Leave by 5:15 AM · Sleep: A-Frame: Milky Way + Desert Views
  • 6:00 AM — South Kaibab Trail · Ooh Aah Point & Cedar Ridge — Orange shuttle from the visitor center; down to Ooh Aah Point (1.8 mi) or Cedar Ridge (3 mi, ~1,150 ft). No water on the trail.
  • Alt: Shadier option: Bright Angel Trail (1.5-Mile Resthouse) to the 1.5-Mile Resthouse.
  • 12:00 PM — Canyon Vistas ride with Canyon Vistas Mule Ride (Xanterra) — ~2 hr in the saddle along the rim. Book now; it opens 15 months ahead.
  • 3:00 PM — Late lunch in Williams: Gateway Sandwich Co. (Tue–Sat 8–4).
  • 4:00 PM — Sauna and rest at the A-frame.
  • 6:30 PM — Dinner: Red Raven Restaurant — the best sit-down in Williams.
  • Alt: Casual swap: Obregón City Tacos for tacos.

Day 10 · Sun, Jun 27 · Sedona — Down Oak Creek Canyon to Soldier Pass, creek time at Crescent Moon

~1h30 Williams → Sedona (Flagstaff, then AZ-89A down Oak Creek Canyon) · Leave by 6:30 AM · Sleep: “Oasis” homestead cottage in Oak Creek Canyon
  • 6:30 AM — Breakfast burritos to go at Anna's Canyon Cafe in Williams (opens 6).
  • 8:00 AM — Park at Sedona Shuttle · Posse Grounds Park & Ride and ride the free shuttle to Soldier Pass Trail (Seven Sacred Pools) — Devil’s Kitchen sinkhole, Seven Sacred Pools (look, don’t swim) and the climb-up cave (~2.5 hr).
  • Alt: Bigger payoff: Cathedral Rock Trail by shuttle — 1.2 mi, 744-ft slickrock scramble. Or walk across Devil's Bridge (Mescal Trailhead).
  • 11:30 AM — Lunch: Outlaw Grille in Uptown — burgers.
  • 1:00 PM — Check in at “Oasis” homestead cottage in Oak Creek Canyon — private hot tub, spring-fed creek and a lawn for the kids. Rest through the heat.
  • 4:30 PM — Crescent Moon Picnic Site (Red Rock Crossing) — wade Oak Creek in the shade with Cathedral Rock reflected in the water.
  • 6:45 PM — Dinner: The Hudson — red-rock views; book the dining room.

Day 11 · Mon, Jun 28 · Sedona — Cathedral Rock at sunrise, Slide Rock, Broken Arrow by jeep

Short hops around Sedona · Leave by 5:45 AM · Sleep: “Oasis” homestead cottage in Oak Creek Canyon
  • 6:00 AM — Cathedral Rock Trail — 1.2 mi, 744 ft of hands-on slickrock to the saddle. Before the heat and the crowds.
  • 7:30 AM — Breakfast at Coffee Pot Restaurant — 101 omelettes.
  • 8:45 AM — Slide Rock State Park — 80-ft natural rock chute in Oak Creek. Water shoes. Lot closes when full.
  • If Slide Rock is full: Grasshopper Point Swimming Area — deep cliff-lined swimming hole ($15).
  • 1:00 PM — Hot tub and rest.
  • 4:00 PM — Pink Jeep Tours · Broken Arrow Broken Arrow tour — the steep slickrock route.
  • Alt: Family-owned swap: Red Rock Western Jeep Tours private tours from $104.95.
  • 6:30 PM — Dinner: Hideaway House — Italian on a creek-view terrace.
  • Alt: Sunset splurge: Mariposa Latin Inspired Grill (4.4, just under the bar), or a sunset at Airport Mesa Overlook.
  • Alt: Shady creek hike instead of the jeep: West Fork of Oak Creek or Fay Canyon Trail.

Day 12 · Tue, Jun 29 · Travel day — Cliff dwellings on the way to Phoenix, fly home

~2h Sedona → PHX (+30 min with stops) · Leave by 9:00 AM · Sleep: Home
  • Alt: Sunrise option: Devil's Bridge (Mescal Trailhead) from the Mescal trailhead before checkout.
  • 10:00 AM — Check out; brunch at Crema Craft Kitchen in Old Town Cottonwood (25 min).
  • 12:00 PM — Montezuma Castle National Monument — 800-year-old cliff dwelling, 30-min paved loop just off I-17.
  • 2:00 PM — Drop the car at Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX); afternoon flight home.
