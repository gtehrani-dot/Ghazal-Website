The Farm
What's actually on-site at Sugar Water Manor, per the host's own welcome message.
On-site

Animal chore-time visits

The host arranges morning and/or afternoon animal visits during daily chore times — a working farm, not a petting zoo, but hands-on for guests who want it.

Fri & Sat nights (other nights by request)

Fire pit & s'mores

Lit before sunset, with a s'mores kit left at check-in. Good for cooking dinner over, too.

Year-round

Pool & kayaks

Both available on-site for guest use throughout the stay.

In The Homestead

Games & puzzles

On hand for a slow evening or a rainy stretch.

Mon–Fri, order by 3 PM

Squeaky's takeout delivery

The Homestead has a standing delivery arrangement with a local restaurant called Squeaky's for evenings after 6 PM.

Daily

Breakfast treat delivery

The host delivers a breakfast treat to the door each morning of the stay.

Also Worth Seeing
Somerset County spots within a short drive — not confirmed as visited on this trip.
~20 min drive

Crisfield, MD

The self-proclaimed "Crab Capital of the World" — working waterfront, seafood restaurants, and the dock where the Smith Island ferry departs.

🧭 Directions
~20 min drive, near Crisfield

Janes Island State Park

Water trails through salt marsh, a boat-access beach, and kayak/canoe rentals — no roads reach the island side, it's paddle-in only.

🧭 Directions
Passenger ferry from Crisfield

Smith Island

Chesapeake Bay's last inhabited offshore island reachable only by boat — famous for the many-layered Smith Island cake, Maryland's official state dessert.

🧭 Directions
~20 min drive

Princess Anne, MD

Somerset County's historic seat — a walkable colonial-era downtown along the Manokin River.
