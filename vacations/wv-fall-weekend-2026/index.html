Day 1 · Monday, October 12

Depart McLean → Blackwater Falls & Lindy Point

🍂 Mid-Oct in the WV highlands: low-mid 50s°F by day, high 30s at night Historical
☕ Breakfast — Drifters Cafe, Wardensville 🥪 Lunch — packed from McLean
On the way
  • Leave McLean by ~7:00 AM — figure 3.5–4 hrs total to the Blackwater Falls area via Rt 55 / US-48 Corridor H.
  • Breakfast — Drifters Cafe, 50 W Main St, Wardensville, WV — open Mon 7am–4pm, fresh-ground coffee and a well-reviewed breakfast burrito. About 1.5 hrs into the drive, right on Rt 55. (304) 490-9797 Directions →
  • Lunch's already sorted — grab sandwiches in McLean before you leave. Handy either way: Davis's own picks (Big Belly Deli, Hellbender Burritos) are both closed Mondays.
Blackwater Falls State Park (late morning)
  • Blackwater Falls Boardwalk / Gentle Trail — paved path + 200+ step boardwalk down to the main falls overlook; starts at the Trading Post parking area. 0.25 mi round trip · easy · ~15–20 min · ★4.7 (1,115 Tripadvisor reviews, park-wide) Directions →
  • Lindy Point Trail — one of the park's best views: the Blackwater Canyon from 3,000 ft. On your saved map. 0.6 mi round trip · easy-moderate · ~20–30 min · ★4.7 (1,115, park-wide — no separate trail listing) Directions →
  • If there's time/energy — Elakala Falls + Balanced Rock Loop — right from the Blackwater Lodge parking lot. Worth flagging: Tuesday's now a full checkout-and-travel day with no window to come back for this, so today's really the only shot at it this trip. Totally fine to skip and save for next time if Lindy Point already did you in. 2.8 mi loop · moderate · 375 ft gain · ~1.5–2 hrs · ★4.7 (park-wide) Directions →
  • Bonus if energy allows — Pendleton Point Overlook (easy, family-friendly, right by the Nature Center) and Pace Point Overlook (along the Canyon Rim Trail from Pendleton Lake) — both on your saved map and in this same corner of the park. Pendleton: <0.2 mi, easy, ~10 min · Pace Point: ~3 mi round trip, moderate, ~1.25–1.5 hrs · ★4.7 (park-wide) Pendleton →Pace Point →

Day 1 (cont.) · Monday, October 12

Check In — Dolly Sods Cabin

🍲 Dinner — cook in
  • 4:00 PM — check in at the cabin, 239 Shamrock Dr, Maysville. Full directions, gate code, and the navigation warning are on the Property Details tab — read it before you leave signal range.
  • 🍲 Dinner — cook in. Kitchen's fully stocked, Weber grill on the back deck.
  • Evening: the private Finnish sauna, accessible right off the deck.

Also on your map — worth a look if you have extra energy

  • Bear Rocks Preserve — 2 mi / ~5 min from the cabin. The hosts' #1 "don't miss" — phenomenal at sunrise, still great in soft morning light. Best fit is a quick pre-breakfast visit Tuesday if anyone's up early before checkout, since Monday's schedule is already full. Sunrise Tuesday, Oct 13 is ~7:23 AM (Petersburg-area estimate, closest reference point) — you'd want to leave the cabin by ~7:05 AM to catch it. ~1 mi easy walking loop among the rock outcrops · ~30–45 min · ★5.0 (5 Tripadvisor reviews — small sample, but it's the hosts' most-repeated pick) Directions →
  • Rohrbaugh Plains Trail (from Fisher Spring Run Trailhead) — the hosts' own personal favorite, to a quiet overlook. About 20 min from the cabin, deeper into the Dolly Sods Wilderness. ~5 mi round trip · moderate-strenuous · ~2.5–3 hrs · no independently verified rating found, but trail guides consistently describe it as quiet/uncrowded Directions →
  • Spruce Knob — West Virginia's highest point (4,863 ft), 360° views from the observation tower. Genuinely great, but 45 min–1 hr from the cabin — really a "next trip" stop given how tight this one is. ~0.5 mi paved loop to the tower · easy · ~15–20 min · no independently verified rating found
  • Stack Rock — on your saved map near this same area, but I couldn't independently pin down trail specifics (distance/difficulty) with confidence. Worth asking locally rather than planning around it sight-unseen.
  • Canaan Loop Trail / Canaan Loop Rd — more of a scenic backcountry gravel loop road (popular with mountain bikers and OHVs) than a hike. Nice if you have a spare hour, not a stop to build a day around.

  • Before 11:00 AM — check out. Full before-you-leave checklist (stove/heat off, lock up, dishes) is on the Property Details tab.
The drive to Stanardsville
  • Drive to Stanardsville, VA (2010 Madison Rd) — about 3–3.25 hrs via US-33 E through Franklin and Harrisonburg, crossing Skyline Drive directly at the Swift Run Gap entrance station (mile 65.5). Directions →
Skyline Drive stop — Dark Hollow Falls
  • Dark Hollow Falls (Skyline Drive mile 50.7, ~15 mi south of Swift Run Gap) — a real 70-ft waterfall payoff, steep on the return climb. With the drive up and back from the gap, budget roughly 2–2.25 hrs total for this stop. 1.4 mi loop · moderate · 440 ft gain · ~1 hr 15 · ★4.5 (576 Tripadvisor reviews) Directions →
  • 🥪 Lunch — Big Meadows Wayside, right there at mile 51: a "Grab 'n Go" counter with sandwiches, salads, pastries and drinks — easiest lunch stop on the whole route. It's a seasonal concession, typically running through fall foliage season, but worth a quick call ahead given how remote this stretch is. Directions →
Check in & evening
  • 4:00 PM — check in to Postcard Cabins Shenandoah, 2010 Madison Rd, Stanardsville, VA 22973. Book via the link below or call (888) 236-2427. Website / book →
  • 🔥 Dinner — grill out. Each cabin has a fire pit on the deck; if you want an actual grill rather than just the fire pit, worth confirming with the property ahead of time or bringing a small portable one.

Getting to the gate

  1. Off paved Jordan Run Rd, turn onto FR 75 (Brushy Ridge Rd) — you'll see signs for Dolly Sods at the turn.
  2. FR 75 turns to gravel after about half a mile. Follow it roughly 2.5 miles to a road on the left: Shamrock Dr.
  3. There's a yellow gate — padlock code 9726. Always lock it behind you, even for a short trip back out.
  4. Take your first right past the gate, head uphill, and the cabin is on the left in about a quarter mile.
