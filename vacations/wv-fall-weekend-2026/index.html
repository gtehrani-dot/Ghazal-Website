The drive to Stanardsville
  • 2:00–2:30 PM — target arrival at the trailhead. Drive toward Stanardsville, VA (2010 Madison Rd) via US-33 E through Franklin and Harrisonburg, crossing Skyline Drive directly at the Swift Run Gap entrance station (mile 65.5). The cabin-to-Harrisonburg leg alone typically runs ~2 hrs 50 min–3 hrs, so with the lunch stop below this is a realistic pace, not a rushed one. Directions →
  • 🥪 Lunch pickup — Mr J's Bagels & Deli, 1635 E Market St, Harrisonburg — right on US-33 as you pass through, sandwiches made to order (never pre-made). Skyline Drive stop — Dark Hollow Falls
  • Dark Hollow Falls (Skyline Drive mile 50.7, ~15 mi south of Swift Run Gap) — a real 70-ft waterfall payoff, steep on the return climb. Picnic with the Mr J's sandwiches at the trailhead or the Big Meadows overlook nearby before or after hiking. 1.4 mi loop · moderate · 440 ft gain · ~1 hr 15 · ★4.5 (576 Tripadvisor reviews) Directions →
  • Backup picnic option — if Mr J's timing doesn't work out, Big Meadows Wayside (mile 51, right by the falls) runs a "Grab 'n Go" counter with sandwiches, salads and pastries. It's a seasonal concession, typically open through fall foliage season, but worth a quick call ahead given how remote this stretch is. Directions →

Getting to the gate

  1. Off paved Jordan Run Rd, turn onto FR 75 (Brushy Ridge Rd) — you'll see signs for Dolly Sods at the turn.
  2. FR 75 turns to gravel after about half a mile. Follow it roughly 2.5 miles to a road on the left: Shamrock Dr.
  3. There's a yellow gate — padlock code 9726. Always lock it behind you, even for a short trip back out.
  4. Take your first right past the gate, head uphill, and the cabin is on the left in about a quarter mile.
