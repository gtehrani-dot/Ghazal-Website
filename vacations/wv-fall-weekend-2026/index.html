The Skyline Drive stop — a routing heads-up
  • Basye sits on the west side of the mountains in the Shenandoah Valley — the direct route to it never touches Skyline Drive. Stopping at Little Stony Man or Dark Hollow Falls means a real detour: east into Shenandoah National Park at Thornton Gap (US-211, near Luray), then back out the same way to continue toward Basye. Budget it as an actual add-on, not a free stop "on the way."
  • Recommended: Little Stony Man (Skyline Drive mile 39.1, ~8 mi north of the Thornton Gap entrance) — 0.9 mi one-way to the cliffs (~1.8 mi out-and-back), easy-to-moderate, sweeping views of the Shenandoah Valley and Massanutten Mountain without the climb up Stony Man itself. Given the detour, this keeps the add-on to roughly 45 min–1 hr total including the walk. Directions →
  • Alternate: Dark Hollow Falls (mile 50.7, ~19 mi further south on Skyline Drive) — 1.4 mi loop, 440 ft gain, moderate (steep on the return climb), a real 70-ft waterfall payoff. Pick this instead if you'd rather see a waterfall than a view — but it adds roughly 30–40 more minutes of driving on top of Little Stony Man's detour, since you're going further into the park before backtracking west. Directions →
