Getting to Como from the airport (train reference)
From Malpensa (MXP): Malpensa Express (Trenord) runs every ~30 min, 4:57am–11:27pm daily, from both Milano Cadorna and Milano Centrale. For Como: (1) ride toward Milano Cadorna, change at Saronno for the Como Nord Lago line (Cadorna–Saronno–Como Lago, ~hourly, ~1 hr) — Como Nord Lago is on the lakefront by the ferry docks; or (2) ride to Milano Centrale and take a Trenord/Trenitalia train to Como San Giovanni (~37 min–1 hr). ~1h30 door-to-door.
From Linate (LIN): no train station — M4 metro (blue line) to San Babila (~15 min), then cross to Cadorna/Centrale for a Como train. ~1h45 door-to-door.
Pull the day's exact times on trenord.it / malpensaexpress.it before travel — minutes shift by day and season. No advance booking needed.
