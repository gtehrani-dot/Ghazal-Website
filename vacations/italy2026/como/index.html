Day-by-Day Itinerary
One base for the whole trip — Filario in Lezzeno — with day trips out to Bellagio and Varenna. Transport alternates between the C130 bus, the lake ferry, and (on arrival/departure day) a private car.
Getting around from Lezzeno: C130 bus (still signed locally as "the C30") to Bellagio ~15–20 min — per the hotel, the stop closest to Filario is "Pescaù – Bivio Bagnana" (also signed "Pescaù – Località Bagnana"), not the Lezzeno–Piazza Roma stop further down the line — far more frequent than Lezzeno's own small ferry stop · Bellagio–Varenna ferry ~15 min, boats roughly every 20–30 min in season (~7:30am–8pm) · Lezzeno → Milan Malpensa by car 65–90 min. Current schedule (Aug 3–30, 2026 reduced-summer timetable, which covers this whole trip): C130 Como–Nesso–Bellagio PDF. C130 tickets: pay the driver on board — cash (small bills/coins) or a contactless card/phone tap (ASF's "SwipeOnLake" system). No app needed, though myCicero is there if you'd rather buy ahead.

🚏 Getting there
  • Arrival flight not yet booked — routing decided: fly into Milan Linate (LIN), chosen for the scenic train approach into Varenna over the Malpensa/Como-town alternative.
  • Leg 1: shared shuttle bus from LIN to Milano Centrale (Linate Shuttle/Air Pullman or Starfly/Airport Bus Express), ~25 min, ~€5–8pp, departures roughly every 20–30 min.
  • Leg 2: Trenord regional train, Milano Centrale → Varenna-Esino, ~1 hr, ~€7.40pp — this is the scenic stretch, hugging the lake shore on the final approach.
  • Leg 3: Varenna–Bellagio ferry, ~15 min, ~€4.60pp (Navigazione Laghi).
  • Leg 4: C130 bus from Bellagio to the Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana stop — confirmed by the hotel as the stop closest to Filario (not the Lezzeno–Piazza Roma stop), ~15–25 min depending on which Bellagio stop you board at.
  • Total door-to-door: roughly 2.5–3 hrs and ~€17–20pp, door-to-door pricing depending on shuttle bus fare — plus whatever the LIN flight itself costs.
🏡 Check-in & dinner
  • Check in at Filario Hotel & Residences, Lezzeno. Breakfast is included for all 4 mornings.
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner: Filo — the hotel's fine-dining terrace, first-night splurge 4.7, $$$.

Tue, Aug 11 · Bellagio
Bellagio + rest
🚌 C130 bus, ~15–20 min from Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana (stop nearest Filario, per the hotel) 🚌 C130 current schedule (PDF)
🌤️ Weather · Temp: 22–29°C (72–84°F) · Precip: low, ~20% chance of a PM storm · Wind: light 5–10 km/h (typical for August)
🍳 Breakfast — included at Filario 🥪 Lunch — not planned 🍽 Dinner — Trattoria San Giacomo
  • Morning: beach and pool time at Filario — recover from travel.
  • 🚤 Optional swap for the morning — ferry loop or private charter: instead of the pool, take an earlier C130 bus to Bellagio, then either (1) hop the central-lake ferry across to Menaggio (~20–30 min crossing, every 20–30 min in season, ~€4.60 one-way or €15.20 day pass covering unlimited Bellagio–Varenna–Menaggio–Cadenabbia crossings, pay at the dock, no booking needed), or (2) a private self-drive boat rental from Bellagio (no license needed, from ~€75/hr, fuel + insurance included) for a more flexible loop past Menaggio and the villas in between. Either way, an hour or so around Menaggio's lakefront, then back to Bellagio in time for the afternoon walk below.
  • Afternoon: C130 bus from Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana (stop nearest Filario, per the hotel) to Bellagio. Good departures for an early-to-mid afternoon start: 1:41pm, 2:06pm, or 2:41pm (Aug 3–30 reduced-summer timetable) — walk Salita Serbelloni, the promenade, Punta Spartivento.
  • Gelato at Gelateria del Borgo, Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 46.
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner: Trattoria San Giacomo 4.4, $ — aim to arrive ~7pm; no reservations taken.
  • Alternate if there's a wait: La Punta / Bar Il Molo — peninsula-tip lake view.
  • Bus home after dinner: C130 from Bellagio–Lido — good options around 8:03pm, 9:00pm, or 9:03pm (yes, that ~55-min gap between 8:05 and 9:00pm is real — confirmed against the official timetable, not a missed departure; if dinner runs long, 7:31pm is the last option before it). Get off at Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana (nearest Filario, per the hotel), a few minutes past the Lezzeno–Piazza Roma stop. Last bus of the night departs Bellagio around 11:00pm (reaches Pescaù ~11:24pm) — don't miss it, there's no later option.

🚏 Getting to MXP
  • Not booking a private car this time — taking the ferry and train out instead, which is both cheaper and part of the scenery. No advance booking needed for any of these legs; pay/tap on board or at the station.
  • Leg 1: C130 bus from Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana to Como (or transfer via Bellagio, whichever the day-of timetable favors) — ~15–20 min to Bellagio, then the lake ferry on to Como town, ~1hr–1.5hrs total depending on which boat connects (Bellagio–Como direct crossings run roughly every 4 hrs; confirm the exact day-of times on Navigazione Laghi close to travel, and always keep the earlier of two viable connections).
  • Leg 2: from the Como ferry dock, it's ~15 min on foot to Como San Giovanni station. Trenord regional train to Milano Centrale, ~40–60 min, ~€5–8pp.
  • Leg 3: Malpensa Express from Milano Centrale to Malpensa T1, ~51 min, ~€15pp, departs roughly every 30 min (5:25am–11:25pm) — no need to pre-book, buy at the station or via the app.
  • Total door-to-door: roughly 3–3.5 hrs and ~€20–25pp — leaving Lezzeno by 9:30am targets arrival at MXP by early-to-mid afternoon, a comfortable buffer ahead of a 3:20pm international departure. Double-check the Bellagio–Como ferry and Como–Milano train times a few days out, since both run on seasonal schedules.
