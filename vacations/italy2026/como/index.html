The Como Region
Where this trip actually sits on the lake, and the three towns that make up the ferry-connected center.

Lezzeno sits on the eastern shore of the Bellagio peninsula — the wedge of land where Lake Como splits into its two southern arms, the Como branch to the southwest and the Lecco branch to the southeast. Bellagio itself perches right at the tip of that peninsula, nicknamed "the Pearl of the Lake" for exactly that reason — it's the one town where you can stand and see all three arms of the lake at once. North of that point, the lake widens into a single upper basin, with Varenna on the eastern shore and Menaggio across on the western shore. All three towns sit on the same Navigazione Laghi ferry network, which is what makes it possible to string them together into a single day without a car — this trip's itinerary already uses two of the three (Bellagio and Varenna); Menaggio comes up as the gateway for the Day 4 Lido di Lenno option, but is worth knowing on its own even if that day goes a different direction.

The peninsula's tip

Bellagio

"The Pearl of Lake Como" — perched right where the lake's two southern arms meet, elegant lanes climbing from the water, the waterfront promenade, Villa Melzi's gardens. This trip's main hub for dinners and daytime wandering — Days 2 and 3 both center here.

Upper lake, east shore

Varenna

Quieter and more colorful than Bellagio — a working fishing-village feel, pastel houses stacked above the harbor, the Passeggiata degli Innamorati (Lovers' Walk) along the water, and Castello di Vezio on the hill above. This trip's Day 3 lunch stop.

🧭 Directions
Upper lake, west shore

Menaggio

The more laid-back of the three — a lakefront promenade, easy onward access to Tremezzo and Cadenabbia's villa gardens (Villa Carlotta, Villa del Balbianello), and a common jumping-off point for hikes into the hills behind town. Not on this trip's day-by-day plan, but it's the gateway for the Day 4 Lido di Lenno option and worth a look if a ferry day heads that way.

Nesso, east shore · ~20 min from Bellagio

Orrido di Nesso — waterfall, gorge & Roman bridge

A dramatic gorge where two streams meet in a waterfall that plunges straight into the lake, spanned by the picturesque Ponte della Civera, a Roman/Romanesque stone bridge. The best view is from the base — descend the cobbled steps (~340) from Piazza Castello down to the bridge. One thing to know: it's best in spring and early summer (April–May) when the falls are running full; by July–August the flow drops and the falls can be nearly dry. Right on the SS583 east-shore road, ~20 min south of Bellagio.
