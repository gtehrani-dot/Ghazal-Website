Lezzeno & Bellagio · 4 days

Lake Como

Filario Hotel private beach, the Lovers' Walk in Varenna, a cooking class above Bellagio, and the best pasta in three towns.

Day by day

Arrive evening of the 10th, depart morning of the 14th — 3 full days between, alternating sightseeing with real rest at the hotel's private beach.

ARREvening

Settle in

  • Check in at Filario Hotel & Residences, Lezzeno.
  • Dinner at Filo $$$$ — the hotel's fine-dining terrace, first-night splurge.
DAY 2Bellagio

Bellagio + rest

  • Morning: beach and pool time at Filario — recover from travel.
  • Afternoon: C30 bus to Bellagio (~10-15 min) — walk Salita Serbelloni, the promenade, Punta Spartivento.
  • Gelato at Gelateria del Borgo, Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 46.
  • Dinner: Trattoria San Giacomo $$ — arrive ~7 PM, no reservations taken.
DAY 3Cooking class

Cook with a View + Villa Melzi

  • Late morning: Cook with a View cooking class at Il Perlo, above Bellagio — includes lunch and Villa Melzi garden tickets.
  • Afternoon: Villa Melzi gardens, then back to the hotel for pool/beach.
  • Dinner: Yeast Side $$ at the hotel, 7 PM — open until 8, casual pizzas and apps on the beach.
DAY 4Varenna

Lovers' Walk + last dinner

  • C30 bus to Bellagio, ferry to Varenna.
  • La Passeggiata degli Innamorati (Lovers' Walk) — starts right at the Varenna ferry dock.
  • Lunch: Il Cavatappi $$$ — small, reserve ahead.
  • Gelato at Gelateria Riva, on the harbor.
  • Ferry + bus back to the hotel.
  • Last-night dinner: Osteria Il Governo 1801 $$-$$$ in Lezzeno — no travel needed, best-rated meal of the trip.
DEP14th

Departure

  • Flight is 3:20 PM out of MXP. Depart the hotel by 11:00-11:30 AM — drive is 65-90 min plus airport buffer.

Getting around

Lezzeno's own ferry stop only gets a handful of boats a day — the C30 bus is the real workhorse.

C30 Bus — Lezzeno ↔ Bellagio

~10-15 minutes, far more frequent than the Lezzeno ferry stop. Confirm current schedule with the front desk at check-in.

Departure transfer — Lezzeno → MXP

Drive is 65-90 min. Water taxi won't save time (MXP is on the far side of Milan from the lake) — book a private car instead. Taxi Lezzeno explicitly serves Filario, fixed price, ballpark ~€180-230.

Hotel

Filario Hotel & Residences — private beach, infinity pool, water-taxi service on request.
