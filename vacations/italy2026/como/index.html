🚏 Getting there
  • Land at Milan Linate (LIN), 3:20pm — the same Aeroitalia flight that closes out the Sardinia leg. Shuttle bus to Milano Centrale (~25 min), then the Trenord train on to Varenna-Esino (~1 hr), tracing Lake Como's eastern shore for the last stretch of the ride.
  • Look to catch the 6:30pm ferry from Varenna across to Bellagio (7:10pm as the backup) — a 15–30 min crossing timed for Lake Como's golden hour, the hills above Bellagio catching the last light as the boat pulls in. It's a short walk down from the Varenna-Esino station to the dock.
  • C130 bus, Bellagio–Lido → Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana (stop nearest Filario) — the 7:03pm departure lines up with the earlier ferry, 7:31pm with the later one, either way about a 20–25 min ride in. Full leg-by-leg pricing is on the Hotel & Transportation tab and the Print tab.
🏡 Check-in & dinner
  • Check in at Filario Hotel & Residences, Lezzeno. Breakfast is included for all 4 mornings.
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner: Filo — the hotel's fine-dining terrace, first-night splurge 4.7, $$$. Open 7–10pm, reservation recommended.
  • If the travel day runs late and the Filo window gets tight, ask Filario at check-in whether Yeast Side (normally closes at 8pm) can do room service from its kitchen until 10pm instead — a same-property fallback rather than losing the night's dinner plan.

🍝 Varenna — lunch first, then the walk
  • Bus + ferry: C130 from Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana at 10:11am, then the Bellagio–Varenna ferry (~15 min crossing, every 20–30 min in season) — puts you in Varenna by ~11:15–11:30am.
  • 🍽 Lunch first: Il Cavatappi 4.6, $$ — arrive right around opening to be seated before the early-afternoon rush; lunch is walk-in, no reservation.
  • La Passeggiata degli Innamorati (Lovers' Walk) — starts right at the Varenna ferry dock, an easy post-lunch stroll.
  • Wander Varenna's old town — narrow lanes down to the harbor, Gelateria Riva for gelato.
  • Optional: the steep 20-min hike up to Castello di Vezio — medieval castle overlooking Varenna, falconry demonstrations. Only worth it if energy allows on top of everything else today.
⛴ Into Bellagio for the evening
  • Ferry back to Bellagio — look to catch the 5:55pm or 6:30pm boat (15–30 min crossing) to leave room before the La DolceVita reservation; boats keep running every 20–30 min well past 11pm if more time in Varenna is wanted.
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner (reserve ahead): La DolceVita 4.0, $$, Salita Antonio Mella 18 — call ahead for a table, no walk-in-line hassle. Open Wednesdays; closed Thursdays.
  • Backup: Yeast Side back at the hotel (casual, no reservation, open till 8pm) if the reservation falls through.
  • Bus home: C130 from Bellagio–Lido, same evening departures as Day 2 (8:03pm, 9:00pm, 9:03pm, last one ~11:00pm) — get off at Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana.

Option A — Cook with a View cooking class

Il Perlo, above Bellagio. Small-group class, includes lunch and Villa Melzi garden tickets. Pickup outside Banca Intesa Sanpaolo, Via Lungo Lario Manzoni 24, Bellagio (green/white VW ID.Buzz van). Book via Viator, product code 254673P16.

Option B — E-bike tour from Bellagio

Bike It! Bellagio runs daily group e-bike tours (min. 2 people) — the Hidden Gems Tour winds past villas and fishing hamlets, or the Ghisallo pilgrimage ride climbs to the Madonna del Ghisallo cycling chapel/museum. Bike rental, English-speaking guide, helmet included. Reachable via the same C130 bus as every other Bellagio day.

Option C — Beach club day at Lido di Lenno, via a Menaggio boat loop

Lido di Lenno, Via Comoedia 1 — sandy beach, loungers/umbrellas/cabanas for rent, restaurant on site, open 10am–1:30am. On the west shore, ~6km from Lezzeno as the crow flies but no direct bus, so get there on the water instead of by road: take the C130 to Bellagio, then either (1) hop the central-lake ferry across to Menaggio (~20–30 min crossing, every 20–30 min in season, ~€4.60 one-way or €15.20 day pass covering unlimited Bellagio–Varenna–Menaggio–Cadenabbia crossings, pay at the dock, no booking needed) and continue on to Lenno, or (2) a private self-drive boat rental from Bellagio (no license needed, from ~€75/hr, fuel + insurance included) for a flexible loop past Menaggio and the villas in between, ending at the beach club. Either way the boat ride there is as much the point as the beach itself — the furthest-flung of the three options, but the most scenic way to get there.

🚏 Getting to MXP
  • Ferry + train back — scenic, affordable option. Buy at the dock/station; NaviTap contactless tap doesn't cover luggage, so it's counter or cash with bags in tow.
  • Leg 1: C130 bus from Pescaù–Bivio Bagnana to Como (or transfer via Bellagio, whichever the day-of timetable favors) — ~15–20 min to Bellagio, then the lake ferry on to Como town, ~1hr–1.5hrs total depending on which boat connects (Bellagio–Como direct crossings run roughly every 4 hrs; confirm the exact day-of times on Navigazione Laghi close to travel, and always keep the earlier of two viable connections).
  • Leg 2: from the Como ferry dock, it's ~15 min on foot to Como San Giovanni station. Trenord regional train to Milano Centrale, ~40–60 min, ~€5–8pp.
  • Leg 3: Malpensa Express from Milano Centrale to Malpensa T1, ~51 min, ~€15pp, departs roughly every 30 min (5:25am–11:25pm) — no need to pre-book, buy at the station or via the app.
  • Total door-to-door: roughly 3–3.5 hrs and ~€20–25pp — leaving Lezzeno by 9:30am targets arrival at MXP by early-to-mid afternoon, a comfortable buffer ahead of a 3:20pm international departure. Double-check the Bellagio–Como ferry and Como–Milano train times a few days out, since both run on seasonal schedules.
