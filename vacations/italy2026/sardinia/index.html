Trip Overview
Everything at a glance — no need to read the full itinerary to answer "when do we land" or "how far is today's drive."
Why this route: One home base — Casagliana Suite Resort in Olbia — for all 4 nights, so there's no mid-trip packing. The three excursion days are sequenced by driving distance, closest first: Golfo Aranci (15 min) the arrival evening, then the Maddalena boat day from Palau (40–45 min), then the longer Orosei charter from Cala Gonone (1h 25–35m), building to the longest drive of the trip — La Pelosa on the far northwest tip (2h 15m) — right before the short hop back to the airport. Each excursion day is built around exactly one activity; nothing is double-booked.
Heads up — La Pelosa now requires a paid online reservation (it didn't when this itinerary was first drafted). Book via the official site 5–7 days ahead for peak season — see Book Now.

Thu, Aug 6 · Arrival
Arrival & Golfo Aranci
🚗 ~15–20 min OLB → Casagliana · leave for dinner by 6:30 PM 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: ~30°C/86°F day, ~19–21°C/66–70°F night · Precip: rare, ~6 rainy days/month · Wind: light (typical for August — trip is beyond the live forecast window)
  • ✅ Flight booked: easyJet Milan Malpensa (T2) → Olbia (T1), departs 10:45 AM, lands 12:05 PM — checked bags included
  • Land at Olbia (OLB) ~12:05 PM, drive ~15–20 min to Casagliana Suite Resort, check in, pool time — no agenda pressure today, the trip's three big days start tomorrow
  • 🏖 Easy beach option, close to the hotel: Spiaggia di Portisco and/or Cala Banana — both ~15 min from Casagliana, a low-key swim for the afternoon before heading into Golfo Aranci for dinner. Cala Banana's shallow, sandy, arched cove is easy with kids and looks out at Tavolara island; Portisco has a small marina with umbrella/kayak rental if you want gear on the spot
  • Evening in Golfo Aranci, a working 19th-century fishing village where boats still dock daily and the harbor still runs on the fishermen's schedule
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner: Pescheria del Golfo ★4.8 — pick your fish straight from the fishmonger's counter and they fry it fresh, served dockside in a paper tray. Casual, cash-and-carry, genuinely local — no reservations taken
  • Alternate: La Vecchia Marina — sit-down version, same daily catch
  • Or, sunset stroll: the Lungomare promenade past the colorful fishermen's houses, the bronze mermaid statue, and the old port — low-key, no crowds, a good reset after travel

Fri, Aug 7 · Maddalena Islands
Maddalena Archipelago boat day
🚗 ~40–45 min Casagliana → Palau · leave by 8:00 AM 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: ~30°C/86°F day, ~19–21°C/66–70°F night · Precip: rare, ~6 rainy days/month · Wind: light (typical for August — trip is beyond the live forecast window)
  • All-day (~7 hr) Maddalena Islands boat excursion — leaving Casagliana by 8:00 AM gets you into Palau ~8:45, with time for breakfast before most operators' mid-morning boarding
  • Recommended operator: Delfino Tours — daily excursions to Spargi, Budelli, La Maddalena & Santa Maria, with a stop at the Pink Beach (Spiaggia Rosa) view
  • 🍽 Breakfast before boarding: Le Dolcezze Napoletane ★4.5 (632 reviews), #1 of 77 in Palau — Neapolitan pastry shop steps from the ferry dock, Via Nazionale 48
  • Alternate: Cafe del Mar — ★4.5, sit-down with sea views
  • 🍽 Can't-miss dinner, back in Palau: CuCumiao ★4.3 (2,500+ reviews) — traditional Gallurese/seafood, handles a full evening crowd well without dropping quality
  • Alternate: O'Belau Ristorante — ★4.4, book ahead
  • If there's daylight left: Santa Teresa Gallura, 20–25 min north of Palau — Piazza Vittorio Emanuele and the old center for a walk, Rena Bianca beach for a look, Capo Testa granite headland toward Corsica at sunset. Otherwise, save it for its own morning

Sat, Aug 8 · Orosei Charter
Cala Luna & Cala Mariolu by boat
🚗 ~1h 25–35m Casagliana → Cala Gonone · leave by 6:45 AM — longest one-way drive on the trip 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: ~30°C/86°F day, ~19–21°C/66–70°F night · Precip: rare, ~6 rainy days/month · Wind: light (typical for August — trip is beyond the live forecast window)
  • The furthest day-trip base on this itinerary, so an early start pays off — leaving Casagliana by 6:45 AM gets you to Cala Gonone ~8:10–8:20, time for breakfast before a typical 9:00 AM charter departure
  • Recommended operator: Palmasera Boat Rental — Piazza del Porto Box 14, visits Cala Luna, Cala Mariolu, and the other calas in the Gulf of Orosei
  • 🍽 Breakfast at the harbor: Bar Puddu ★4.6 — technically attached to a gas station, don't let that fool you: garden setting, sweeping Gulf of Orosei views, excellent coffee and paninis you can take onto the boat. Viale Colombo 1
  • Alternate: Garden Bar — ★3.8, central, simpler
  • 🍽 Lunch after the charter: Il Nuovo Gabbiano ★4.1 (1,555 reviews) — right on the water, order the catch of the day off a working fishing harbor
  • Alternate: La Poltrona — ★4.2, good backup if Gabbiano's full
  • Pack reef/water shoes for the calas — rocky entry at Cala Mariolu

Sun, Aug 9 · Stintino
Spiaggia La Pelosa
🚗 ~2h 15m Casagliana → Stintino · leave by 6:30 AM — biggest driving day, ~4.5 hrs round trip 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: ~30°C/86°F day, ~19–21°C/66–70°F night · Precip: rare, ~6 rainy days/month · Wind: light (typical for August — trip is beyond the live forecast window)
  • La Pelosa sits on the far northwest tip of the island — the furthest point on this itinerary. Leaving by 6:30 AM puts you on the beach ~8:45, ahead of both the midday heat and the crowds
  • ⚠️ Requires an online entry reservation — timed QR-code entry, 8 AM–6 PM, €3.50 pp, daily cap ~1,500 visitors. Book via spiaggialapelosa.it (the only authorized site) 5–7 days ahead in peak season
  • 🍽 Coffee on arrival: Bar I Ginepri — terrace bar right over the beach, good for a coffee and pastry before claiming a spot on the sand
  • 🍽 Easy lunch, beachfront: Bar I Ginepri terrace — panini and light Sardinian plates, steps from the water, no need to leave the beach
  • Alternate: La Pelosetta — ★3.6, fuller sit-down menu (seafood pasta, wood-oven pizza)
  • Sun protection — shade is limited on the beach and worth having in the car for the long drive back

Spiaggia La Pelosa

The far northwest tip of the island — white sand, shallow turquoise water, views to the Asinara and Piana islets and the old Aragonese tower. Requires a paid online reservation, timed 8 AM–6 PM entry, book 5–7 days ahead in peak season.
