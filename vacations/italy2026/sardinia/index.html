The five days

  • Thu 8/6 Land ~11 AM, check in, easy afternoon, evening stroll + fry-fresh dinner in Golfo Aranci (~15 min).
  • Fri 8/7 All-day (7 hr) Maddalena Islands boat excursion from Palau (~40–45 min). Leave by 8:00 AM.
  • Sat 8/8 Orosei charter to Cala Luna & Cala Mariolu from Cala Gonone (~1h 25–35m). Leave by 6:45 AM.
  • Sun 8/9 Spiaggia La Pelosa, Stintino (~2h 15m) — the far northwest tip. Leave by 6:30 AM.
  • Mon 8/10 Departure. OLB is ~15–20 min from the hotel — leave by 12 or 1 PM depending on flight time.

Notes worth flagging

  • Pacing Days 2–4 are each built around one excursion — no double-booking a beach + a boat in the same day.
  • La Pelosa ~4.5 hrs of driving round trip, plus the beach caps daily visitor numbers in peak season — worth checking if online booking is required before you go.
  • Santa Teresa Sits 20–25 min past Palau — pairs naturally as a golden-hour stop on the way back from Maddalena if the group has the stamina.
  • Fresh catch Golfo Aranci's pescheria-friggitoria format (pick it, they fry it, you eat it dockside) is unique to that town on this route.

Optional — only if you extend the trip

  • Santa Barbara Trail 500km mining-history hiking route in Sulcis-Iglesiente, SW Sardinia — ~3+ hrs from Casagliana. Beautiful, but a different trip.
  • Sinis Peninsula Cycling near Oristano, west coast — ~2–2.5 hrs from Casagliana.
  • Tuerredda Beach Often ranked among Sardinia's best beaches, deep south near Teulada — ~3+ hrs from Casagliana.
  • Reachable now Capo Testa (Day 2 add-on) and a Golfo Aranci coastal walk (Day 1) cover the "explore on foot" idea without the extra driving.
Thursday, August 6

Arrival & Golfo Aranci

~15 min drive
Leave for dinner: 6:30 PM
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)

Land at Olbia around 11 AM, drive straight to Casagliana (roughly 15–20 min from the airport), check in, unpack, pool time. No agenda pressure today — the trip's three big days start tomorrow. In the evening, head to Golfo Aranci, a working 19th-century fishing village where boats still dock daily and the harbor still runs on the fishermen's schedule.

Dinner
Pescheria del Golfo
★ 4.8 (Restaurant Guru)
Pick your fish straight from the fishmonger's counter — calamari, mussels, shrimp, tuna, whatever came in that morning — and they fry it fresh and serve it dockside in a paper tray. Casual, cash-and-carry, genuinely local.
Alt: La Vecchia Marina — sit-down version, same daily catch. lavecchiamarina.com
Or, sunset stroll
Golfo Aranci harborfront
Free
The Lungomare promenade past the colorful fishermen's houses, the bronze mermaid statue, and the old port — low-key, no crowds, a good reset after travel.
Friday, August 7

Maddalena Islands

~40–45 min drive
Leave by 8:00 AM
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
All-day · 7 hr excursion

The boat excursion runs about 7 hours, so this is the one true "all-in" day. Leaving by 8:00 AM gets you into Palau around 8:45, with time for breakfast before most operators' mid-morning boarding. Expect stops at Spargi, Santa Maria, and views of the Pink Beach (Spiaggia Rosa) across the archipelago.

Breakfast — before boarding
Le Dolcezze Napoletane
★ 4.5 (632 reviews) · #1 of 77 in Palau
Neapolitan pastry shop steps from the ferry dock — cornetti, cannoli, strong espresso, quick enough to grab and go before the boat. Via Nazionale 48, Palau.
Alt: Cafe del Mar — ★4.5, sit-down with sea views.
Dinner — back on land
CuCumiao
★ 4.3 (2,500+ reviews)
Solid Italian/seafood back in Palau for whenever the boat gets in — handles a full evening crowd well without dropping quality.
Alt: O'Belau Ristorante — ★4.4, book ahead.
If there's daylight left
Santa Teresa Gallura — 20–25 min north of Palau. Worth the detour if the group still has legs after 7 hours on the water: Piazza Vittorio Emanuele and the old center for a walk, Rena Bianca beach for a look, and Capo Testa for granite headland views toward Corsica at sunset. Otherwise, save it for its own morning.
Saturday, August 8

Orosei charter — Cala Luna & Cala Mariolu

~1h 25–35m drive
Leave by 6:45 AM
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
Longest one-way drive on the trip

This is the furthest day-trip base, so an early start pays off. Leaving Casagliana by 6:45 AM gets you to Cala Gonone around 8:10–8:20 — time for breakfast at the harbor before a typical 9:00 AM charter departure.

Breakfast — at the harbor
Bar Puddu
★ 4.6 (Google)
Technically attached to a gas station — don't let that fool you. Garden setting, sweeping views over the Gulf of Orosei, excellent coffee and paninis you can take straight onto the boat. Viale Colombo 1, Cala Gonone.
Alt: Garden Bar — ★3.8, central, simpler.
Lunch — after the charter
Il Nuovo Gabbiano
★ 4.1 (1,555 reviews)
Right on the water in Cala Gonone — order the catch of the day off a working fishing harbor, not a tourist stand-in.
Alt: La Poltrona — ★4.2, good backup if Gabbiano's full.
Sunday, August 9

Spiaggia La Pelosa, Stintino

~2h 15m drive
Leave by 6:30 AM
🌤️ Weather · Temp: — · Precip: — · Wind: — (Forecasts open up ~10 days out)
Biggest driving day — ~4.5 hrs round trip

La Pelosa sits on the far northwest tip of the island — the furthest point on this itinerary from Casagliana. Leaving by 6:30 AM puts you on the beach around 8:45, ahead of both the midday heat and the crowds — La Pelosa caps daily visitors in peak season, so an early arrival matters more here than anywhere else on the trip.

Coffee, on arrival
Bar I Ginepri
Mixed but consistent praise for the view
Terrace bar right over the beach — good for a coffee and pastry before you claim a spot on the sand.
Lunch — beachfront
Bar I Ginepri (terrace lunch)
Beautifully presented, view of La Pelosa
Panini and light Sardinian plates, steps from the water — easy, no need to leave the beach.
Alt: La Pelosetta — ★3.6, fuller sit-down menu (seafood pasta, wood-oven pizza). lapelosettarestaurant.it

If 2:00 PM flight
Tight but relaxed
Depart hotel by 12:00–12:15 PM

If 3:00 PM flight
Room for one last stop
Depart hotel by 1:00–1:15 PM
Same slow morning, but enough slack for a quick last coffee or pastry stop in Golfo Aranci (~15 min detour) on the way to the airport before the ~90 min buffer.
