Sun, Aug 9 · Stintino
Spiaggia La Pelosa
🚗 ~2h 15m Casagliana → Stintino · leave 8:30–9:00 AM — biggest driving day, ~4.5 hrs round trip 🧭 Send route to Google Maps
🌤️ Weather · Temp: ~30°C/86°F day, ~19–21°C/66–70°F night · Precip: rare, ~6 rainy days/month · Wind: light (typical for August — trip is beyond the live forecast window)
  • La Pelosa sits on the far northwest tip of the island — the furthest point on this itinerary
  • ⚠️ Leaving later (8:30–9:00 AM) means arriving ~10:45–11:15 AM — past the ~8–9 AM window locals use specifically to beat the parking crunch; the main lot (~320 car spaces, €2–2.50/hr) reportedly fills by ~10 AM in August. The beach itself isn't at risk (1,500-visitor cap via reservations, entry open until 6 PM), but if the lot's full, overflow is roadside parking further back along the access road toward Stintino — up to ~2 km away, a 20–25 min walk in full midday sun (~30°C). A quick taxi from Stintino village is the easiest way to skip that walk if it comes to it
  • ⚠️ Requires an online entry reservation — timed QR-code entry, 8 AM–6 PM, daily cap 1,500 visitors, €3.50 pp (kids under 12 free, not counted in the reservation). 500 of the 1,500 spots can be booked any time with no advance-window limit (even months out, and for a run of consecutive days); the remaining 1,000 release 48 hrs before each date, opening at 8:00 AM two days prior. Book the no-limit allocation now rather than gambling on the 48-hr release in peak season. Book via spiaggialapelosa.it (the only authorized site)
  • 🍽 Coffee on arrival: Bar I Ginepri — terrace bar right over the beach, good for a coffee and pastry before claiming a spot on the sand
  • 🍽 Easy lunch, beachfront: Bar I Ginepri terrace — panini and light Sardinian plates, steps from the water, no need to leave the beach
  • Alternate: La Pelosetta — ★3.6, fuller sit-down menu (seafood pasta, wood-oven pizza)
  • Sun protection — shade is limited on the beach and worth having in the car for the long drive back

Golfo Aranci Lungomare

Day 1 · Right in the village, 10-min walk from the harbor

Cinque Spiagge

Golfo Aranci's own five numbered town beaches — Prima, Seconda, Terza, Quarta & Quinta Spiaggia, running along the seafront right in the village. Sandy and calm, no drive needed if you're already in town for the evening.

Day 1 · Easy afternoon swim, ~15 min from Casagliana

Spiaggia di Portisco

North toward the Costa Smeralda between Porto Rotondo and Porto Cervo — low, sandy bottom with a few outcropping rocks, a small marina alongside, and umbrella/kayak rental on site if you want gear without packing your own.

Cala Banana

An arched cove of fine white sand right between Olbia and Golfo Aranci — shallow, sandy seabed makes it easy with kids, with views out to Tavolara island. Street parking, small kiosk on site.
