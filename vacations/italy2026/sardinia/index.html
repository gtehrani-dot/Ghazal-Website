Beaches & Coves
The full list from the itinerary, gathered in one place — worth having on hand if plans shift day to day.
Day 4 · Stintino

Spiaggia La Pelosa

The far northwest tip of the island — white sand, shallow turquoise water, views to the Asinara and Piana islets and the old Aragonese tower. Requires a paid online reservation, timed 8 AM–6 PM entry, €3.50 pp. The no-limit allocation is gone/sold out — booking now only happens via the 48-hr release, opening 8:00 AM two days before your visit date.

Day 2 · Optional trip out, ~35 min from Casagliana

Spiaggia del Principe

One of the Costa Smeralda's signature beaches — Romazzino area near Arzachena, white sand and turquoise water framed by pink granite. Surfaced from the host's guest guide. A real drive compared to the Day 1 beaches, so treat it as the one outing on an otherwise slow day.

Day 3 · La Maddalena Archipelago (catamaran tour stops)

Cala Corsara & Cala Santa Maria

Typical stops for this style of Maddalena catamaran day tour: Cala Corsara on Spargi (white granite, turquoise water, ~2 hrs) and Cala Santa Maria on Santa Maria Island (~1.5 hrs), plus a swim/snorkel stop at the Porto Madonna natural pools. Most tours also sail close by Budelli's famous Pink Beach for photos, but landing and swimming there has been banned since 1994 to protect the sand — view-only from the boat. Exact stops depend on the operator and weather; confirm the day-of plan with Sardinia Catamaran.

🌐 Website
Not on this trip's itinerary · Caprera

Cala Coticcio

The hero photo on this page — but worth flagging: it's not a stop on the Day 3 catamaran tour. It's reachable only by a permitted guided hike from Caprera (40–60 min, steep and rocky, no shade) or by boat with an operator holding the right park permit to anchor nearby — landing on the beach itself is hike-only. The daily visitor cap fills within hours and guided slots can sell out weeks ahead, so this would need its own separate booking if you want to actually visit rather than just admire the photo.

Day 3 add-on · Santa Teresa Gallura

Rena Bianca

Fine white sand right in Santa Teresa's old town, 20–25 min north of Palau — an easy pairing with the Maddalena boat day if there's still daylight, otherwise its own morning.

Cinque Spiagge

Golfo Aranci's own five numbered town beaches — Prima, Seconda, Terza, Quarta & Quinta Spiaggia, running along the seafront right in the village. Sandy and calm, no drive needed if you're already in town for the evening.

Spiaggia di Portisco

North toward the Costa Smeralda between Porto Rotondo and Porto Cervo — low, sandy bottom with a few outcropping rocks, a small marina alongside, and umbrella/kayak rental on site if you want gear without packing your own.

Also Worth Seeing
Researched, real options that didn't make this trip's four days — too far from Casagliana for a day trip, or skipped in favor of a slower pace, but worth knowing about for a future visit.
Michelin option · San Pantaleo (~35 min)

Ristorante Il Fuoco Sacro

The Michelin-star alternative to Parco degli Ulivi for a splurge night — modern Mediterranean tasting menus (~€100+/pp) from the Enrico Bartolini group, at Petra Segreta Resort with a stunning panoramic view over the archipelago toward La Maddalena and Corsica. 4.6 Google. We chose Parco degli Ulivi for Day 2 (à la carte, kid-friendly, better value) and a sunset drink here instead — but this is the pick if you ever want the full Michelin dinner.

Skipped this trip · Gulf of Orosei, near Cala Gonone

Orosei Bay Boat Charter — Cala Luna & Cala Mariolu

Officially skipped for this trip in favor of a slow Day 2 back at Casagliana — kept here for a future visit. ~1h 25–35m drive, Casagliana → Cala Gonone, the longest one-way drive on the itinerary. Recommended operator: Palmasera Boat Rental, Piazza del Porto Box 14, Cala Gonone — visits Cala Luna (arched sea caves, pine trees down to the sand, reachable only by boat or a long hike) and Cala Mariolu (round white-pebble beach beneath limestone cliffs, striking turquoise-to-deep-blue water, rocky entry — bring water shoes), plus the other calas in the Gulf of Orosei. Breakfast at the harbor before a typical 9:00 AM departure: Bar Puddu ★4.6 (alternate: Garden Bar, ★3.8). Lunch after at Il Nuovo Gabbiano ★4.1 (1,555 reviews), right on the water (alternate: La Poltrona, ★4.2).
