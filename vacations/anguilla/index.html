Transportation

Flight — United UA 346, IAD → St. Martin (SXM)

Departed Washington Dulles 8:41am, Fri Mar 27. Fly into St. Martin, then taxi to the ferry.

Flight — United UA 1223, St. Martin (SXM) → Washington

Departed 3:50pm, Thu Apr 2 — the return flight home.

Ferry — Blowing Point ⇄ Marigot

Two options used on this trip: Calypso Charters (private, 1:45pm or 2:45pm pickup near the SXM airport) on arrival, and the public ferry from Blowing Point to Marigot (~30 min from the airport) on departure day.

Rental car

Picked up on arrival (Wendell's Car Rental was the arrival-day contact), returned at Bowling Point on the way out.
Itinerary
Day 1 · Fri, Mar 27 · Arrival
Shoal Bay East
  • Fly into St. Martin (SXM) on UA 346, taxi to the ferry
  • Ferry to Anguilla via Blowing Point — Calypso Charters at 1:45 or 2:45pm, or Wendell's car rental met us there
  • Drive to Manoah Hotel, check in
  • 🍽 Breakfast/lunch options: Roti Hut or Tropical Sunset (conch salad)
  • 🍽 Dinner: SandBar, 7:30pm (per calendar — actual booked reservation)
Have the printed Calypso ticket ready. Flight was UA 346, IAD departing 8:41am.
Day 2 · Sat, Mar 28 · Beach day
Sandy Ground → Sandy Hill
  • 🍳 Breakfast: Le Bon Pain
  • Beach hop from Sandy Ground Beach to Sandy Hill
  • 🍽 Lunch: grilled lobster at Sandy Hill
  • 🍽 Drinks at 20 Knots, then dinner reservation at Veya, 7:30
Day 3 · Sun, Mar 29 · Beach day
Shoal Bay East
  • 🍳 Breakfast: Tasty's or at the hotel
  • Beach
  • 🍽 Lunch options: Gwen's Reggae Bar and Grill (for the vibe), Tasty's, Roti Hut, or Blanchards
  • 🍽 Dinner: Sharky's, 6:15 (reservation needed) — live music Sundays
  • Night kayaking, 8:15pm
Day 4 · Mon, Mar 30 · Change of base
Manoah → Tranquility Beach
  • Check out of Manoah
  • 🍳 Breakfast: yogurts & croissants
  • Drive the island / waterpark
  • Check in to Tranquility Beach
  • 🍽 Dinner: Tokyo Bay – Aurora Anguilla Resort, ~7:45–8:45pm (per calendar — the plan shifted here from the originally requested Jacala's to Tokyo Bay)
Day 5 · Tue, Mar 31 · West End
Cap Juluca / Maundays Bay
  • Beach day at Cap Juluca
  • 🍽 Lunch: Valleys (to go) or Sunshine Shack (no reservation needed)
  • 🍽 Dinner: Tokyo Bay, 7:15pm (confirmed per calendar, over the Sandbar alternate)
  • Photos on the beach
Day 6 · Wed, Apr 1 · Beach day
Tranquility Beach
  • 🍳 Breakfast: hashbrowns and eggs at the condo
  • Beach
  • 🍽 Lunch: Straw Hat
  • 🍽 Dinner: Mango's — reservation 6:30
Day 7 · Thu, Apr 2 · Departure
Return trip
  • Check out, return rental car at Bowling Point
  • 🍳 Breakfast: yogurts & pastries
  • Public ferry to Marigot (~30 min from the airport)
  • Flight home: UA 1223, SXM departing 3:50pm
