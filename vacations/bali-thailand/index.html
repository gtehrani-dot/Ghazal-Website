Transportation

Outbound: United 932 + Thai Airways

United 932 Dulles → Frankfurt (9:56pm–11:40am); Thai Airways 921 Frankfurt → Bangkok (2:10pm–6:20am); Thai Airways 431 Bangkok → Denpasar (8:40am–2:00pm).

Bali → Chiang Mai (via Bangkok)

Air Asia FD3678 Denpasar → Bangkok (11:55am–3:15pm); Air Asia FD3238 Bangkok → Chiang Mai (6:35pm–7:40pm).

Chiang Mai → Phuket (via Bangkok)

Air Asia FD3231 Chiang Mai → Bangkok; Air Asia FD3025 Bangkok → Phuket (10:50am–12:15pm) — connecting into the sailing charter.

Rental car — Bali

Self-drive for the Bali leg, ~$350.
Itinerary
Bali — Seminyak & Ubud
Feb 11–16
  • Sunrise ~6:21am, sunset ~6:45pm this time of year
  • Seminyak base at the W, then a self-drive loop: Tanah Lot → Kuta → Uluwatu → Jimbaran → Nusa Dua → Sanur → Nusa Lembongan → Bali Orchid Garden
  • Ubud leg at the Chedi Club — rice terraces, temples, the slower half of the Bali stay
Chiang Mai
Feb 16–19
  • Based at U Chiang Mai Hotel in the old city
  • Northern Thai food and temple-hopping before flying south to the islands
Sailing the Andaman Sea — Phuket to Railay
Feb 19–22 · 3 nights aboard a private charter
  • Sunrise ~6:45am, sunset ~6:35pm
  • Islands and stops: Ko Mai Thon, Ko Naka Yai, Maya Bay (Phi Phi Lei), Ton Sai Bay (Phi Phi Don), Loh Samah Bay, Hin Klang
  • Meals, accommodations, and excursions included in the charter price
Railay Beach, Krabi
Feb 22–24
  • Two nights in Railay Beach Village — the climbing-town beach base, accessible only by longtail boat
Bangkok & departure
Feb 24–25
  • One-way flight Krabi → Bangkok
  • Final night at the St. Regis, near Lebua Tower and the Banyan Tree Bangkok for a last drink
  • Depart for home Feb 25
The Sailing Leg
The boats that were compared before booking the charter.

Faraway — Mozart

Catamaran, large with a big net deck. Free massage, Italian espresso, snorkeling, kayaks included.

Faraway — Oasis

Catamaran, similar amenities to the Mozart.

Faraway — Full Steam

Catamaran, good condition, snorkeling & kayak included.

Island Cruises — Full Steam

Catamaran, kayak included.

Yacht Pro — Jeanneau Sun Odyssey 32i

Monohull, well-reviewed, Railay drop-off included in price.

Blue Water Sailing

Monohull, good reviews, does not depart from Phuket directly.

Island Cruises — Nakamal

Monohull, kayak included.

Lanta (Wyn) — Chameleon

Catamaran option considered for the same route.
