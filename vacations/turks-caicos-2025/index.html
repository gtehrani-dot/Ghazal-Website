Beaches & Reef
Grace Bay's calling card is right outside the door — plus the reef snorkel spot a short walk from Coral Gardens.
Right outside Coral Gardens

Grace Bay Beach

12 miles of powder-white sand and calm, shallow turquoise water — consistently ranked among the best beaches in the world. No entry fee, no booking needed.

🧭 Directions
Turtle Cove, ~10 min from Coral Gardens

Smith's Reef

Shore-entry snorkeling inside Princess Alexandra National Park — coral heads, sea turtles, and rays in shallow, protected water. Bring your own gear or rent locally; no reservation needed.

🧭 Directions
