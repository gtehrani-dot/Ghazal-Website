Day-by-Day Itinerary
Valley and Glacier Point days run from one base in Oakhurst, the Tioga Road crossing is its own travel-and-play day, and two nights at Convict Lake cover Mammoth before the drive north to Tahoe. Hard days (Mist Trail, Tioga) sit between easier ones.

  • 4:30 PM — Check in at Convict Lake Resort — Spa Cabin (about 40 min south). Ask for the spa cabin with its own outdoor hot tub.🧭 Directions📞 760-934-3800
