Day-by-Day

Day 1 · Mon, Aug 2 · Travel day — Fly into Fresno, giant sequoias, settle into Oakhurst

~1 hr FAT → Oakhurst · 25 min to Mariposa Grove · Sleep: Yosemite Pink Cottage
  • Fly DC → Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT). No nonstop from DC; aim to land by about 1 PM.
  • Pick up the one-way rental at the terminal rental-car center (drop at RNO after Tahoe).
  • 1:30 PM — Lunch: Sam's Italian Deli & Market — sandwiches and Italian deli counter, about 15 min from the airport.
  • Alt lunch: Heirloom, farm-to-table in north Fresno, right on the CA-41 route.
  • 2:30 PM — Drive about 1 hr up CA-41 to Oakhurst.
  • 3:45 PM — Mariposa Grove of Giant Sequoias — park at the Welcome Plaza and take the free shuttle; walk the 2-mi Grizzly Giant loop. Last bus down was 8 PM in 2026.
  • 6:30 PM — Check in at Yosemite Pink Cottage (Airbnb). Hot tub tonight; sauna is for the 13-year-old and up, short supervised sessions.
  • 7:15 PM — Dinner: Mountain Oaks Cafe — chili verde, pozole, chowder; one of the few Oakhurst spots open Monday.

Day 2 · Tue, Aug 3 · Yosemite Valley — Mist Trail to Vernal Fall, then the Merced River

~1h30 each way Oakhurst ↔ Valley · Leave by 6:30 AM · Sleep: Yosemite Pink Cottage
  • 6:20 AM — Donuts and coffee to go at Judy's Donuts.
  • 7:50 AM — Tunnel View — the first view of the Valley; 10 minutes is enough.
  • 8:30 AM — Mist Trail to Vernal Fall (Happy Isles) — 2.4 mi round trip, 1,000 ft of granite steps through the spray. Bring rain jackets; no permit needed for Vernal Fall.
  • Alt: Easier version: turn around at the Vernal Fall footbridge (1.6 mi, 400 ft).
  • 12:15 PM — Lunch: Curry Village Pizza Deck — the Valley’s best-rated food, steps from the trail.
  • 1:30 PM — Lower Yosemite Fall Loop — flat 1-mi loop to the base of North America’s tallest waterfall.
  • 2:30 PM — Swim and wade at Cathedral Beach (Merced River) with El Capitan overhead. NPS: only when the river is low and calm; never above a waterfall.
  • Or Sentinel Beach (Merced River), calmer and shallower with picnic tables.
  • Or swap the swim for Mirror Lake Trail — Half Dome reflections, though the lake can be low by August.
  • 4:45 PM — Valley View (Gates of the Valley) on the drive out.
  • 7:00 PM — Dinner splurge: Elderberry House (Château du Sureau) — seasonal tasting menu. Call to confirm kids are welcome.
  • Alt: Casual swap: South Gate Brewing Co. (below the 4.5 bar, very kid-easy).

Day 3 · Wed, Aug 4 · Glacier Point Road — Taft Point, Sentinel Dome and Glacier Point, then a slow afternoon

~1h15 Oakhurst → Glacier Point Rd trailheads · Leave by 9:00 AM · Sleep: Yosemite Pink Cottage
  • 8:00 AM — Breakfast at Bee's Bakery Cafe; order sandwiches to go for lunch.
  • 10:15 AM — Taft Point & Sentinel Dome Trailhead — Taft Point first (2.2 mi): cliff-edge Fissures with 2,000-ft drops, so hold hands. Then Sentinel Dome (2 mi, 400 ft) for the 360° summit view.
  • 1:00 PM — Picnic at Glacier Point, then Washburn Point for the side view of Vernal and Nevada falls.
  • 3:30 PM — Back at the cottage: hot tub, sauna, nothing scheduled.
  • 6:00 PM — Dinner: Smokehouse 41 BBQ — Oakhurst’s favorite BBQ, Wed–Sun only, no reservations; go early.
  • Alt: Treat: Reimer's Candies & Ice Cream for ice cream.
  • Alt: Sunset instead: take a picnic dinner back up to Glacier Point for sunset (~8 PM) and skip Smokehouse (it’s closed the rest of your Oakhurst nights).

Day 4 · Thu, Aug 5 · Tioga Road — Across the high country to Mono Lake and Convict Lake

~3h30 of driving before stops (Oakhurst → Lee Vining → Convict Lake) · Leave by 7:30 AM · Sleep: Convict Lake Resort — Spa Cabin
  • 7:00 AM — Breakfast and picnic snacks to go from Bee's Bakery Cafe; check out of the cottage.
  • 9:45 AM — Olmsted Point — the back side of Half Dome and a field of glacier boulders for scrambling (0.25-mi walk).
  • 10:15 AM — Swim at Tenaya Lake (east beach) — cold, clear and sandy on the east end. No rentals here; kids under 13 need a PFD on any paddle craft.
  • 11:45 AM — Pothole Dome — a 1-mi granite scramble with a Tuolumne Meadows view.
  • Alt: Bigger option: Lembert Dome (~3 mi, 850 ft) — off the dome before the afternoon storms.
  • Alt: Longer option: Elizabeth Lake (4.8 mi, 900 ft) to a lake under Unicorn Peak.
  • Alt: Quick snack stop: Tuolumne Meadows Grill (seasonal; soft-serve).
  • 12:45 PM — Over Tioga Pass (9,943 ft). Optional: Gaylor Lakes right at the pass — alpine lakes and old mine ruins.
  • 1:30 PM — Late lunch: Whoa Nellie Deli — fish tacos and lobster taquitos at the gas station that became a legend.
  • 3:00 PM — Mono Lake South Tufa — the 1-mi tufa-tower loop; kids are free.
  • Alt: Add Panum Crater next door — a young volcanic crater rim walk (1.5 mi).
  • 4:30 PM — Check in at Convict Lake Resort — Spa Cabin (about 40 min south). Ask for the spa cabin with its own outdoor hot tub.
  • 6:30 PM — Dinner on site: Restaurant at Convict Lake — reserve ahead.

Day 5 · Fri, Aug 6 · Mammoth Lakes — Rainbow Falls and Devils Postpile, then paddle Convict Lake

~20 min Convict Lake → Mammoth; shuttle to Reds Meadow · Leave by 7:15 AM · Sleep: Convict Lake Resort — Spa Cabin
  • 7:30 AM — Breakfast at The Stove.
  • 8:30 AM — Reds Meadow Shuttle (Mammoth Adventure Center) from the Main Lodge to Reds Meadow.
  • 9:15 AM — Rainbow Falls & Devils Postpile — the basalt columns, then 1.3 mi down to Rainbow Falls (101 ft, often with a rainbow in the spray). Little shade; bring water.
  • 12:45 PM — Lunch in town: Stellar Brew & Natural Cafe — bowls, smoothies, sandwiches.
  • 2:00 PM — Paddle at Convict Lake Marina — kayaks, pedal kayaks or SUPs (2-hr minimum; renter 18+). Then the flat 3-mi lake loop if legs are willing.
  • Alt: Instead: Crystal Lake (Lake George) (3.2 mi, 685 ft) — Mammoth’s best short hike.
  • Or ride the Mammoth Panorama Gondola to the 11,053-ft summit (kids 12 and under free).
  • Or a quick look at Hot Creek Geological Site — boiling turquoise pools, overlook only.
  • 5:00 PM — Hot tub back at the cabin.
  • 7:00 PM — Dinner: Skadi — Nordic-alpine, the best meal in Mammoth. Book ahead.

Day 6 · Sat, Aug 7 · Drive to Tahoe — June Lake breakfast, Bodie ghost town, north on 395 to Lake Tahoe

~4h30–5 hr of driving (Convict Lake → Bodie → Kings Beach) · Leave by 7:30 AM · Sleep: Brockway Springs condo, Kings Beach (Tahoe)
  • 7:30 AM — Check out of Convict Lake Resort — Spa Cabin; drive the June Lake Loop (CA-158).
  • 8:15 AM — Breakfast at Silver Lake Cafe (Silver Lake Resort) on Silver Lake.
  • Alt: Quick swim stop: June Lake Beach ($10/vehicle) or a walk to Parker Lake.
  • Alt: Coffee-only swap: Latte Da Coffee Cafe in Lee Vining.
  • 10:30 AM — Bodie State Historic Park — a gold-rush town left as it was in the 1940s; 1.5–2 hrs. $8 adults, $5 ages 4–17.
  • 12:45 PM — Soak at Travertine Hot Springs — free natural travertine pools a 1-min walk from the car. Heads-up: clothing-optional bathers are common. Skip it if that’s a no.
  • 1:45 PM — Lunch: Coachwhip Cafe in Coleville — tri-tip sandwiches and quiche (Thu–Sun).
  • Or Walker Burger a few minutes north — classic burger stand with outdoor tables.
  • 4:45 PM — Arrive Brockway Springs condo, Kings Beach (Tahoe) in Kings Beach. The Tahoe page picks up from here.
