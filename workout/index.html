No-Equipment Strength Program

A 5-day, no-equipment strength rotation for weight and muscle gain — built for women 40+ and kept Crohn's-friendly. Glute work is called out throughout, and every move links to a form video.

Women 40+ Weight & Muscle Gain Crohn's-Friendly 🍑 Glute-Focused
Day 1Upper Body & Core
Squats 3 × 12–15 Watch
🍑 Elevated Hip Thrusts (2 sec squeeze) 3 × 12–15 Watch
Push-Ups (your progression) 3 × 8–15 Watch
Doorframe / Towel Rows 3 × 10–12 Watch
Plank 3 × 30–60 sec Watch

Rest or light walk

Day 5Full Body & Core
Step-Ups (stairs or sturdy chair) 3 × 10/leg Watch
Pike Push-Ups 3 × 8–12 Watch
Tricep Dips (chair/couch) 3 × 10–12 Watch
Dead Bugs 3 × 10/side Watch
Side Plank 3 × 20–30 sec/side Watch
Day 6–7Recovery
Rest / active recovery
Keep in mind
  • +300–500 cal/day surplus for weight and muscle gain
  • ~0.7–1g protein/lb bodyweight
  • Progress via reps, tempo, or pause — not just added weight
  • Ease off during flares rather than skipping entirely
  • 48 hrs rest between sessions hitting the same muscle groups
