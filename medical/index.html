What this covers. Every analyte with two or more time points across your chart history — grouped into Crohn's / inflammation markers, the GI-MAP stool microbiome panel, vitamins & minerals, cholesterol & cardiometabolic, liver & kidney (relevant to medication monitoring), and thyroid. The shaded band on each chart is the normal reference range; points outside it are marked in red with an H/L flag. Single-reading analytes are listed as tiles at the end of their section instead of a line chart. Hover any point for the exact value, date, and source. Every underlying report this is built from is indexed, with its portal/location, in the Lab Results tab.
Reference ranges shown are the lab-reported ranges from each source at the time of the draw and may vary slightly between labs (e.g. Quest vs. hospital lab). Values pulled from Johns Hopkins MyChart, Inova MyChart, Speer Primary Care (via Hushmail/Quest Diagnostics), and Rupa Health (Dr. Afrouz Demeri). This is a personal tracking view, not a diagnostic tool — bring trends you want to discuss to your next visit with Dr. Charabaty or Dr. Speer.
